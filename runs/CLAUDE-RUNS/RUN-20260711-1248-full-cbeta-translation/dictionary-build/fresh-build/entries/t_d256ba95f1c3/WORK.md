## Graphs, scene, and corpus bend

- graph composition: 十字, cross-shaped/four-way; 街頭, street intersection or street-front
- ordinary scene: a public crossroads where several roads and the traffic on them meet
- Chan bend: the records repeatedly oppose the crossroads to the solitary peak and put answers, activity, noise, public exposure, and unrestricted movement there. They do not give it one fixed favorable value: some masters say it cannot be trodden, others place unrestrained activity there, and later addresses criticize both peak isolation and formulaic crossroads display.
- modifier/material control: not applicable; no material modifier
