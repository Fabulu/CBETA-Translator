# Lane C entries 7–10: independent corpus draft

- `盡大地`: “the whole earth,” used as the maximal spatial field of a claim; exclude occurrences only nested in `山河大地`.
- `山河大地`: “mountains, rivers, and the great earth,” the landscape/world-totality named as a visible collective; do not collapse it into the shorter `盡大地` entry.
- `尊宿`: an established senior teacher, used for living or earlier figures whose standing is already recognized; distinguish the common noun from book titles.
- `宗門`: the lineage/school domain and its characteristic business or vocabulary; titles containing the graphs are not standalone semantic evidence.
