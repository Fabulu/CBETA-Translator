# Public feedback 001 — independent lexicographic review

Date: 2026-07-15

Scope: independent adjudication of the first public feedback on `鳥道`, `玄路`, and `金鎖`, including the process-wide implications. This report distinguishes the reader's valid diagnosis from conclusions that still require corpus support.

## 1. 鳥道

Verdict: **accept the core defect; qualify the proposed formulation.**

The old calque “bird path” did not tell an English reader what scene the expression evokes and could falsely suggest a marked route on the ground. The ordinary scene must be stated: a bird's course through open air leaves no fixed ground track on which a later traveler can place a foot. This directly supports “trackless flight-course.”

The stronger claim “a route that cannot be followed,” or an absolute “non-path path,” does not survive the corpus. The records repeatedly use travel predicates such as `行鳥道`, and there is also a distinct, concretely traversed topographic use for a precipitous mountain trail. Therefore “non-path path” may serve as a reader-oriented search probe, but not as the displayed definition or as a claim of absolute untraversability.

Current repair assessment: the two-sense entry is conceptually sound:

1. a bird's trackless flight-course;
2. a precipitous trail fit for birds.

Required propagation: re-test `三路` and `玄路`, and ensure no dependent prose still treats `鳥道` merely as an unexplained calque or as literally impossible to travel.

## 2. 玄路

Verdict: **accept fully as a searchability defect.**

An entry displayed as “hidden road” must remain retrievable under ordinary English equivalents a reader may reasonably choose. For this sense, the controlled retrieval family is:

- modifiers: hidden, dark, mysterious;
- road nouns: road, path, way, route.

These are retrieval aliases, not twelve separate translation claims and not a global equation of every `道` with “the Way.” Exact Chinese and preferred-target matches must rank above aliases.

Current repair assessment: the entry data now contains the required controlled aliases. The repair is incomplete until the actual website and desktop search are tested to confirm that `SearchAliases` are indexed and ranked.

## 3. 金鎖

Verdict: **accept the material-impression criticism; reject an automatic symbolic replacement.**

“Golden lock” naturally suggests to an English reader either a lock made of gold or a lock with a visibly golden property. The headword's graphs alone do not establish either claim. The reader is right that the old wording smuggled in material composition.

It does not follow that gold therefore symbolizes enlightenment, value, attainment, or anything else. Negative evidence against material composition cannot prove a positive symbolic code. The corpus-supported minimum is the complete referent and its governing predicates: an ornate lock/barrier in palace and pass imagery, plus a separate binding chain/fetter where incompatible animal-and-binding verbs require a different object. The modifier remains unresolved beyond the conventional ornate register unless stronger evidence is found.

Current repair assessment: standalone `金鎖` now avoids the false material calque and splits lock-barrier from chain/fetter. The dependent `金鎖玄路` entry remains stale: its preferred target and alternates retain “golden lock,” it has no search aliases, and its explanation still opens with “built from golden lock.” It must be revised under the family-propagation gate. Re-test `玄關`, `凡情聖見`, and `無事人` as dependents.

## Process-wide consequences

These three reports expose reusable classes of defect rather than isolated mistranslations:

1. **Calque-without-scene:** a graph-by-graph English phrase can be literally tidy while producing the wrong mental picture. Every concrete image, idiom, saying, institution, and opaque compound needs graph composition, ordinary scene/referent, and attested Chan deployment kept distinct.
2. **Correct-but-unfindable:** lexical correctness and retrieval correctness are separate release gates. Every sense needs controlled natural lookup probes, stored aliases, and an end-to-end UI test.
3. **Modifier-to-material leakage:** material/color-looking graphs require an explicit evidence classification; English must not imply construction material without direct support.
4. **Unsupported-symbolism rebound:** correcting a false literalism must not be accomplished by inventing a symbolic meaning.
5. **Predicate-driven sense reopening:** incompatible verbs and physical constraints require a renewed split audit.
6. **Family propagation:** correcting a component reopens every dependent compound and linked explanation.
7. **Corpus-earned opening:** the first sentence must state the warranted meaning and Zen deployment; quotations support the answer but cannot substitute for it.

## Required whole-termbase audit

Audit every existing entry, prioritizing concrete images, opaque compounds, idioms, and apparent material/color modifiers. For each sense record:

- the ordinary scene or referent and its load-bearing constraint;
- the corpus predicates that license the displayed conclusion;
- material/modifier classification where applicable;
- contradictory and literal-control searches;
- 3–5 reader lookup probes and their stored aliases;
- dependent entries reopened by any correction;
- end-to-end search results for preferred, alternate, and alias queries.

The governing guide's rules 11–18 now encode these requirements. Mechanical enforcement and a durable per-entry audit receipt are still necessary; prose rules alone are not completion.
