## 漁父 — under-split; rebuild sense structure

- The single preferred target **“the fisherman”** does not cover all corpus referents. The entry's claim that bare 漁父 has only one person-type and that only the larger compound 漁父詞 supplies a title is contradicted by multiple exact bare-title witnesses:
  - `J/J34/J34nB304.xml`, `0385a18–0385a20`: `<head>漁父</head>` heads a poem beginning with moonlight, reed flowers, a fishing boat, waves, and cormorants.
  - `J/J37/J37nB392.xml`, `0641a22–0641a24`: another independent `<head>漁父</head>` heads a fisherman poem.
  - `T/T47/T47n1996.xml`, `0707a06–0707a08`: another `<head>漁父</head>` heads a verse about a fishing skiff, line, and catching a great fish.
  - Further bare heads occur in `T/T47/T47n2000.xml`, `X/X65/X65n1278.xml`, `X/X69/X69n1347.xml`, and `X/X71/X71n1423.xml`. `J/J34/J34nB311.xml` also uses `《漁父》` explicitly as a work title alongside `《九章》`, `《天問》`, and `《卜居》` (`0699b07`).
- Item 8 therefore requires at least a distinguishable **work/poem-title sense** anchored separately from the fisherman figure/type. “Fisherman figure” versus “work or poem titled *Fisherman*” are different things and can be distinguished by the preferred targets alone.
- The non-title pool also contains a particular classical dialogue figure, not merely a generic occupation: `T/T48/T48n2004.xml`, `0254b12–0254b15`, identifies Qu Yuan and says he addressed the fisherman; `J/J34/J34nB299.xml`, `0183a24–0183a25`, pairs “Qu Yuan and the Fisherman” and says both are wrong; and `T/T48/T48n2016.xml`, `0560c01–0560c04`, gives a speaking fisherman in the “Confucius asks the way” story. Re-adjudicate whether the Qu Yuan Fisherman and the Confucius-story fisherman are particular literary figures requiring one or more figure senses distinct from the generic fisherman type. At minimum the present assertion that generic and literary uses are one referent is not defensible: the contexts point to identifiable story participants.
- Preserve the strong #0g material already present (seat-opening song, courage/staff contrast, guiding-to-waves formula, and impossible nesting), but distribute occurrences under the corrected referents. The inherited “freedom outside doctrine” rejection remains correct.
