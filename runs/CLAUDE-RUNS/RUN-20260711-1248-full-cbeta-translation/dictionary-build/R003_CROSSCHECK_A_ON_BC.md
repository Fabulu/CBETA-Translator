Scope: read-only review of 猢猻, 蝦蟆, 龍蛇, and 木雞 against the allowlisted concordance, guide §5 items 6–9, the nested-term rule, and #0g. No entry files were changed.

## 猢猻 — CORRECTIONS

- The two-sense split is supported: the staged/answering monkey and a human directly called a tailless monkey are different referents. The rejection of the inherited “monkey-mind” psychology is also sound.
- Move or exclude the `南嶽磨磚，猢猻伎倆` occurrence from the human-epithet sense. Here 猢猻 modifies “tricks” in the larger compound “monkey tricks”; it appraises a maneuver, but the bare headword does not denote a human target. The two direct `無尾猢猻` witnesses still independently anchor the human sense, so this correction does not collapse the split.
- The family audit omitted `猢猻子`, a material variant/family at 40 hits in 25 texts. Add it to the ledger and adjudicate whether it adds a unique deployment; do not silently treat it as already covered by the 476/135 bare count.
- Otherwise the public-answer, pillar/bag, four-tail, and drum-performance evidence supports the animal/image definition and #0g account.

## 蝦蟆 — CORRECTIONS

- The creature/image versus direct human-epithet split is supported. “This dead toad,” the caught-toad comparison directed at a respondent, and the forehead-marked-toad exchange independently establish the human target; the draft correctly avoids projecting that appraisal onto every toad occurrence.
- The prose says that after the in-corpus definition of “toad Chan,” the speaker asks why it remains uniformly rough. The stored KWIC ends at `何苦？何苦？` and does not anchor the following unique clause `何苦一味莽鹵？`. Extend that exact occurrence through the clause (and update its line bounds) or remove the claim. Since the clause is part of the direct definition on which the prose relies, extension is preferable.
- Complete the nested-family ledger for `蝦蟆口` (21/16) and `蝦蟆衣` (3/3). They are larger compounds and do not automatically create bare-headword senses, but item 9/nested-term QA requires an explicit keep/exclude decision rather than omission.
- The inherited “one-jump/complacent croaker” gloss was correctly rejected in favor of the text's actual rain-and-croak definition.

## 龍蛇 — CORRECTIONS

- The mixed-assembly versus creature/comparison split is defensible: `龍蛇混雜，凡聖同居` explicitly supplies the community deployment, while `龍蛇易辨，衲子難瞞` explicitly contrasts dragons/snakes with a patched-robed monk. The draft correctly rejects the unproven fixed equation “dragon = adept, snake = unripe.”
- Move `千日禪期今日畢，杖頭卓處活龍蛇` out of the “mixed Chan assembly” sense. Its immediate continuation asks whether any are “leaping and soaring”; it animates dragons/snakes at the staff-tip and does not establish a mixed community. It belongs under the creature/image sense, or should be excluded if that sense already has sufficient non-duplicate evidence.
- The family ledger missed the calligraphic cluster: `筆走龍蛇` 4/4, `龍蛇筆` 2/2, `龍蛇字` 1/1, and `龍蛇勢` 1/1. These are larger phrases, so do not split the bare headword merely because they exist; document them and test whether any passage independently uses bare 龍蛇 as shorthand for calligraphic strokes. Absent that exact shorthand, exclude them under the nested-term rule.
- `龍蛇陣` is itself substantial (56/38). The draft anchors one example but does not report its family count. Add the count and explicitly retain it as a larger formation compound under the creature/comparison family rather than allowing it to blur the mixed-assembly definition.

## 木雞 — CLEAN

- One sense is correct. The still-person comparisons, midnight crowing, soundless crowing, dawn announcement, running with an egg, and sweating all retain the same wooden-rooster image; no person/title referent was found (`木雞和尚`, `木雞禪師`, and direct definition-formula checks are zero).
- Depth is appropriately scaled: `木雞啼` alone is 78/40 within the 128/69 headword concordance, and the eight anchors preserve the dominant Fengxue answer plus distinct impossible predicates and the quieter comparison use.
- The draft correctly revises rather than repeats the inherited Zhuangzi/cock-training explanation: the allowlisted corpus supports comparisons such as `似木雞` (4/4) and `養若木雞` (1/1), but does not make that outside story the definition.
- `王木雞` occurs once only as a personal name string embedded in a poem (`表表神姿王木雞`), not as an independently established title or master named by the bare headword. The WORK adjudication is sound.
