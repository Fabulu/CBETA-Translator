# S001-B sense research

Applied guide §5 #0–#0g, especially item 8: split different referents, never merely different readings. Every saved witness is allowlisted normalized body text and was verified with UTF-8 `zc.py`.

- 本分事: corpus self-gloss and public questions support the Chan “fundamental matter”; `比丘持戒是本分事` independently denotes duty proper to a monastic role. Split; ordinary sense provisional.
- 下語: public encounter sources consistently mean offering a response/capping phrase. B25n0143 is allowlisted body prose (not apparatus) and uses `下語用字` for wording. Split; literary sense provisional because single-source.
- 無位: rankless Chan figure/answer/rank-system language differs from `有德無位`, lack of recognized office. Split; institutional sense provisional.
- 任運: ordinary predicate use differs from the exact `任運第七` oxherding-section title. Two allowlisted J23 texts anchor the title.
- 律: precept/code uses differ from `音律`, regulation of sound or meter. Two lamp recensions and independent B25 body prose anchor the latter.
- 迦葉: Mahakashyapa and Kashyapa Buddha are different figures. The latter is defined only through the masters' deployment in Baizhang's wild-fox case.
