## Provenance leads required by guide §5 item 9

- **QA** — `REQUESTED_TERMS.md`, “GATE 2 — PUBLIC INTERVIEW,” plus completed `問答`, `徵`, `機緣`, `代語`: inherited lead is that questions, answers, probes, substitutions, and verdicts are the public machinery of the tradition.
- **SEAT** — completed `拂子`, `拄杖`, `上堂`, `小參`, `陞座`: inherited lead is that implements and gestures at the teaching seat are institutional acts, not decorative objects.
- **INST** — completed `清規`, `和尚`, `方丈`, `室中`: inherited lead is that offices, rooms, and assembly procedures must be translated by their Chan institutional use.
- **LAW** — completed `勘辨`, `賓主`, `棒`, `喝` and the guide's legal/military family: inherited lead is that interrogation, judgement, command, capture/release, and blows supply the record's public verdict grammar.
- **LINE** — completed `法嗣`, `衣鉢`, `傳燈`, `血脈`: inherited lead is that family and transmission words often denote lineage relations rather than ordinary kinship.
- **FLY** — guide #0g plus completed `鼻孔`, `賊`, `老婆`, `棒`, `葛藤`: inherited lead is that ordinary body, household, trade, and insult words acquire recurrent observable jobs in Chan encounters.
- **CASE** — completed public-case entries and their occurrence/RelatedTerms inventories: inherited lead is the literal case object or phrase, to be tested against its later quotation and comment history without turning it into a riddle.
- **BUD** — completed `佛`, `世尊`, `釋迦`, `迦葉`: inherited lead is that Buddhist figures and words are defined by their Zen deployment, including questions, seat cases, rebukes, and deflations.

“Keep” means the inherited lead survived this concordance screen. “Revise” means the candidate survives but its eventual article must test an ordinary/institutional or literal/loaded sense split instead of inheriting one fused gloss.
