## 老僧 — t_12e8cba30de6

### Sense audit

The current split between “this old monk” and “an old monk” fails item 8. Both denote the same thing, an old monk; first-person self-reference, third-person reference, definiteness, and direct address are grammatical/pragmatic readings, not different referents. Merge those two corpus-wide senses into one, with context-sensitive targets such as “this old monk,” “I, this monk,” “an old monk,” and “the old monk.”

Conversely, move the existing `J/J34/J34nB300.xml` occurrence (`明得空劫自己謂之老僧，也出不得者裏。`) into a separate, narrowly labeled sense: **“the self before the empty eon”** (local definition; MasterName remains null unless attribution is independently established). Here 老僧 names a different thing, not a human monk. It must not be generalized corpus-wide.

After that reorganization the five ordinary-human occurrences already present become one sense, and the local conceptual occurrence stands alone. Add these five human-reference witnesses to bring the ordinary sense itself to 10 and the entry total to 11.

1. **Speaker's teaching locus: “at this old monk's place.”**

   - lbs: `0360a20`
   - KWIC: `老僧者裏即易見難識。若能會得，天下橫行。`
   - English: “At this old monk's place it is easy to see but hard to recognize. If you can understand it, you may travel freely under heaven.”
   - Unique contribution: anchors the productive 老僧者裏 construction as the master's present teaching locus, rather than merely noting its count.

2. **Self-designation used tactically in a public interview.**

   - English: “An old monk ordered two disciples to carry him in a litter. On the way someone asked, ‘Since you are a monk, why are you carrying a monk?’”

   - English: “On Mount Shishuang there was an old monk named Puhui. Although he had conduct of the Way, people of the world did not honor him.”

**Family decision:** define the ordinary sense as a human-role noun with a conventional humble first-person deployment in Chan records. Relate it to 和尚, 山僧, 衲僧, and 僧 without conflating those terms. Keep the exceptional “self before the empty eon” sense local and provisional.
