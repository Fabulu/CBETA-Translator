# 金剛王寶劍 research

- Depth: 271 hits / 111 allowlist texts. Searched Linji's original, the full four shouts, later quotations, public “how is” questions, direct answers, appraisals, and extension to the staff.
- Included four distinct classes: original classification, later comment, direct public answer, and implement-extension. Repeated lamp copies and ornamental sword imagery lacking the Linji classification were excluded.
- Family check: the original roster is sword / crouching golden-haired lion / probing pole and shadow grass / a shout not functioning as a shout. Compatible with 一喝, 四喝, 探竿影草, 踞地師子, and the staff entry.
- Sense test: one corpus-wide thing, the named sword-classification; applying it to shout and staff is an attested extension of function, not a second independently lexicalized referent.
- #0g: a jeweled weapon is bent into a public classification for a shout and then for the teaching-seat staff.
