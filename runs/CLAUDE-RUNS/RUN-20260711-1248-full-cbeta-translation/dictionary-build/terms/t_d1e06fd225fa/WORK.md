- Two corpus-wide senses: institutional chamber interview first; ordinary spatial entry second.

## L004-A item-8 ledger and full retest (2026-07-13)

- sense-target-distinguishability: KEEP “enter the chamber for an interview” versus “enter a room.” Pair 1–2 distinguishes access to the master's institutional interview setting from ordinary spatial entry for dying or fetching a book.
- Family retest: “enter and ask,” “enter and present understanding,” “during a chamber interview,” “chamber-entering disciple,” chamber tests, lineage heir, master's quarters, and sleeping room can all remain true without turning every room entry into an interview or every interview into succession.
- Definition retest: the interview sense remains primary because question, report, repeated test, and disciple-status morphology converge on an institutional referent. Literal movement remains separately proven by two following actions unrelated to interview: sitting upright and dying, and fetching a named book.
- #0g retest: Chan bends ordinary room entry into controlled access to a master's chamber for public-accountable questioning, presentation, and testing, and into the status label “chamber-entering disciple.” The entry reports access and setting without claiming automatic approval or realization.
- Depth repair: added four distinct witnesses—enter-and-ask, disciple status, Ciming's repeated chamber test, and literal entry to fetch a book. Final depth is eight anchors across eight source texts, meeting the 2,365-hit floor and broad source-spread gate without duplicate recensions.
