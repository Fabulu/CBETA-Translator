## Public-feedback and falsification gate

- feedback-observations: code and record witnesses place beds, doors, bells, assigned places, robe-and-bowl sets, people entering, and people standing before or inside the hall; Xuansha Shibei names it alongside stream and buddha hall.
- feedback-inference-verdict: LICENSED — “monks’ hall” is a physical communal building, not an imported figurative category.
- feedback-falsification-searches: 僧堂前, 入僧堂, 僧堂內, 僧堂門, 僧堂牀榻, bell and seat formulas, self-definition questions, and figurative-looking uses.
- feedback-counterexamples: the ‘not the monks’ hall’ answer is a local interview reply and does not erase the hundreds of spatial and regulatory building predicates.
- feedback-scope: physical-building referent corpus-wide; local interview handling remains speaker-specific.
- lookup-probes: monks’ hall, monastic hall, assembly hall for monks, monks’ dormitory hall, enter the monks’ hall, in front of the monks’ hall.
- opening-interpretation-verdict: PASS — building and physical predicates lead.
- plain-english-image-verdict: the ordinary bridge is a communal hall that can have doors, beds, assigned places, and people moving through it.
- nested-compound-verdict: front, inside, door, bell, and bed compounds specify parts or locations, not senses.
- verb-frame-verdict: enter, return to, close, arrange in, sit in, and ring before all require the same building.
- family-definition-retest: the added regulations witness still fits one physical-building sense.
- omission-audit: the seventh witness meets the frequency floor and adds ordination-seniority arrangement.
