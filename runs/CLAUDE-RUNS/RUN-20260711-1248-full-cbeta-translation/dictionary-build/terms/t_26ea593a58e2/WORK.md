## Definition, nested-family, and sense audit

- sense 1: **to descend from the teaching seat**. Formal address close, wordless close, responsive departure, command, preparation to descend, and forcible removal all require the same concrete motion away from the seat. Speech before the descent is a separate act and is not attributed to the descender when the chief seat speaks it.
- sense 2: **the lower seating position**. The `上座下座` (“upper seat or lower seat”) exchange follows a question about southern or northern bowing and explicitly contrasts two seating places. It is a nominal referent, not a departure action. Only one clear allowlisted witness was found, so the sense is provisional.
- nested-compound exclusion: `下座句` is a “lower-seat phrase” inside an upper/lower/not-upper-not-lower wordplay. It cannot buy the headword's action sense or create an additional phrase-title sense without independent evidence.
- family comparison: `陞座` is the mounting counterpart; `上堂` names the formal ascent-to-the-hall event; `便下座` is the common immediate-close construction; `下座歸方丈` adds the return to the quarters; `請下座` is a command. These remain mutually compatible after the noun split.
- sense-target-distinguishability: **KEEP both.** “To descend from the teaching seat” denotes an action; “the lower seating position” denotes a place in an upper/lower seating pair. Neither target paraphrases the other, and each has its own exact occurrence.
