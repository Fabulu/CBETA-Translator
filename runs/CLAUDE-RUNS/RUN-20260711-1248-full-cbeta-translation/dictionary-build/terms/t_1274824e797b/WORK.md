## Semantic remediation r002

- feedback-inference-verdict: SPLIT — the earlier entry noticed `普請看` but mislabeled it a figurative extension. Corpus grammar shows a different requested act: everyone is summoned to look, not to labor.
- feedback-observations: Regulations define equal shared labor and describe placards, signals, exemptions, and work sites; a separate 181-hit formula tells the assembly to beat the drum and look at lamps, moon, world, or the matter raised.
- feedback-falsification-searches: Re-tested work-definition formulas, placards, attendance, field work, hoeing, mill pulling, tea picking, `普請看`, `普請諸人看`, visual objects, and named hall-address uses across 1,567 headword hits in 282 texts.
- feedback-counterexamples: Different labor assignments remain one institutional summons, but collective looking cannot be glossed as labor without falsifying the commanded act. The second sense is therefore required rather than optional interpretation.
- feedback-scope: Sense 1 is the monastery's all-community labor summons; sense 2 is a public invitation for everyone to look. Neither is a noun/verb grammar split.
- lookup-probes: Work searches include `communal work`, `monastery work call`, `community labor`, `all-hands labor`, and `work summons`; viewing searches include `come and see`, `everyone look`, `call everyone to look`, `beat the drum and look`, and `all eyes here`.
- opening-interpretation-verdict: REPAIRED — each sense now names the requested event in its first sentence.
- plain-english-image-verdict: PASS — one scene gathers the community at a work site; the other beats the drum and turns everyone's eyes toward what is being shown.
