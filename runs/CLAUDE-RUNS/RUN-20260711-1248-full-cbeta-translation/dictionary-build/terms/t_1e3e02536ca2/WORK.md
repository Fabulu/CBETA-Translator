# 疑團 research

- Depth: 301 hits / 127 allowlist texts. Searched definition formulas, 一箇/一個, 滾作/打作, 打破, 不破, bodily extensions, autobiographical deployment, and the 疑情/大疑/話頭 family.
- Included five distinct classes: formation as one mass, breaking, intensification under blows, extension through ordinary actions, and the explicit whole-body/unbroken description. Duplicate reprints and repeated late formulas were excluded.
- Family check: narrower than 疑情 (the feeling/condition of doubt) because 疑團 specifically gives it consolidated mass grammar. No evidence contradicted “mass of doubt.”
- Sense test: one thing. “Lump,” “ball,” and “mass” are readings of the same figurative object, not separate referents.
- #0g: the deviation is attested material grammar—doubt is rolled together, bodily comprehensive, unbroken, and broken through—not a claim about spiritual purpose.
