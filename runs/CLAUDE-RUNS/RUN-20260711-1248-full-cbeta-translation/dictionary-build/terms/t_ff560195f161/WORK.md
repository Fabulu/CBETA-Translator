# 作麼生會 research log

- Allowlist concordance: 1,570 hits in 244 files (`zc.count`). Formula searches and the top-file spread were reviewed.
- Deployment inventory: a master asks an individual how he understands a cited action/saying; a master asks the whole assembly after an exposition; the student's answer is immediately tested by a shout or rejection; later case comments reopen an old exchange with the same question.
- Family cross-check: 作麼生, 作麼生道, 會, 不會, and 問答. The phrase asks for the respondent's account in a public exchange; it is not a private comprehension-state or an instruction in a technique.
- Sense test: all reviewed uses are the same interrogative act, “how do you understand it?” Different cited cases and different replies are readings/objects of the question, not different things. One sense retained.
- #0g: ordinary “understand” is bent into a recurrent public-interview demand. The curated set anchors assembly address, individual testing, rejection after a shout, a whisk-case interpretation, later case commentary, and a reply recycled back to its speaker.
- Omission audit: no direct `X者` definition exists or is expected for a full interrogative phrase. Seven nonduplicate deployment anchors were retained; duplicate lamp retellings were excluded.

## Public-feedback and falsification gate

- feedback-observations: named speakers ask after a whisk action, shout, bright-pearl saying, staff address, wind-and-banner saying, and the Puyan–Samantabhadra case; answers are accepted, rejected, or pressed further.
- feedback-inference-verdict: LICENSED — “How do you understand it?” demands an answer about the immediately preceding object. Answer-and-rejoinder structure rules out merely silent inward comprehension.
- feedback-falsification-searches: 你/汝/諸人/如今 plus the headword, preceding objects, following replies, silence/shout/rejection continuations, and rhetorical uses.
- feedback-counterexamples: some uses address an assembly rather than one person, but the public demand is unchanged; different cases supply objects, not senses.
- feedback-scope: interview question corpus-wide; object and verdict local.
- lookup-probes: how do you understand it, what do you make of it, how do you understand this, how do you take it, what is your understanding, how does the assembly understand.
- opening-interpretation-verdict: PASS — English question and public function lead.
- plain-english-image-verdict: this is an interrogative formula; the unstated “it” is recovered from the preceding saying, action, or case.
- nested-compound-verdict: pronominal and temporal frames alter addressee or timing, not sense.
- verb-frame-verdict: ask, answer, reject, shout, remain silent, and press further belong to one question frame.
- family-definition-retest: the added Yunju Shun witness preserves one sense and adds the fourth source.
- omission-audit: individual and assembly address, action, saying, case, rejection, and further testing are represented.
