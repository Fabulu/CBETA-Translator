Succession and a book are different things, not noun/verb grammar or capitalization variants.
- The free phrase is Zen-loaded lineage language, not ordinary lamp hardware. It occurs with an unbroken chain of more than seventy generations, continuing the flame, separate teaching regions, ancestral portraits, and successive transmission.
