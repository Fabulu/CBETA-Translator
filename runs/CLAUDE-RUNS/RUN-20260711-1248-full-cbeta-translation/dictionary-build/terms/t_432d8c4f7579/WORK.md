# Dead saying (死語) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 99 hits in 42 texts.
- Formula sweep: one early record enumerates `死語` among named categories; later records directly ask whether a dead saying can bring people alive. No stable separate literal referent was found.
- Deployment classes retained: classification in an early sayings record, biographical challenge, criticism of receiving an old live function as dead speech, and a later reversal that explicitly speaks of dying under dead words and coming alive.
- Text-drawn relations: 活語, 死法, 活用, 生死語, 死中忽自活.
- Sense test: `死語` always names speech/sayings; opposing evaluations and the later reversal are readings of the same thing, not separate referents. Noun/attributive syntax does not create a different thing here.
- Family cross-check: compared 死句, 活句, 活語, 死法, and 死言死語. Kept the narrower target “dead saying,” with cross-links.
- Omission audit: four anchors cover early taxonomy, later biography, reception of old cases, and explicit inversion. Additional late criticism repeats these classes.
