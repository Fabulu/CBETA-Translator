# When the crazed mind stops, that is awakening (歇即菩提) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 38 hits in 26 texts.
- Definition/formula sweep: the recurring full quotation is “the crazed mind does not stop; when it stops, that is awakening.” Another passage explicitly begins “as for ‘when it stops, that is awakening’…” and gives a Buddhist explanatory expansion; it is reported as that text's gloss, not adopted as the entry's doctrine.
- Deployment classes retained: full quotation, explicit historical explanation, hall address followed by a public question, and a later hall address that asks the assembly whether they can stop.
- Semantic correction: the isolated roadmap target “ceasing itself is enlightenment” drops the grammatical subject supplied by the stock full line and wrongly universalizes all ceasing. Preferred target restores “the crazed mind” and renders awakening rather than imported “enlightenment.”
- Family check: compared stop/rest (歇), rest and cease (休歇), let it rest and cease (休去歇去), crazed mind (狂心), and awakening (菩提). The phrase is a fixed quotation, not a definition of every occurrence of 歇.
- Sense test: quotation, explanation, and hall redeployment all retain the same proposition; no different referent or word class requires a split.
- Omission audit: four anchors preserve source formula, explicit gloss, public challenge, and later redeployment without padding recensions.
