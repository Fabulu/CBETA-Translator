# 弄精魂 research

- Concordance: 215 hits in 108 allowlisted texts.
- Definition/deployment sweep: the enumerative `說宗說教...說禪說道...說心說性` passage is the richest in-corpus boundary and is anchored. Further classes: rebuke of a reading; offered words/displayed understanding; agent noun `弄精魂漢`; criticism of guarding a dead corpse.
- Genre spread: Yuanwu's case commentary, collected comments, and later teaching records. Two Yuanwu occurrences remain because they anchor different targets of the phrase.
- Family cross-check: compared standalone `精魂`, `錯會`, `呈見解`, `守屍鬼`, and `昭昭靈靈`. The family does not warrant defining 精魂 as a doctrine or consciousness-substance. The literal object remains, while the construction's consistently disparaging deployment is stated.
- Sense test: one action, including its productive agent noun. Different things criticized are objects of the rebuke, not distinct meanings of the headword.
- #0g: an ordinary “play with” construction is bent into a recurrent Chan verdict across speech, comments, and fixed conduct. The entry surfaces that observable deployment and stops short of stating a hidden intent.
