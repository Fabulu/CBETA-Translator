## Discovery provenance and disposition

- Source: `DISCOVERY_3.md` row 2, carried through `REQUESTED_TERMS.md` and `REQUESTED_BUILD_PLAN.md` r003-C.
- Inherited lead: not merely muddling through, but a master deliberately extends an opponent's mistaken move to expose it. Motivating line: “with no road to turn, he could only follow one error with another.”
- Disposition: **revise and keep.** Keep the clean action “follow one error with another” and the attested deliberate case continuations. Reject “to expose it” as a universal intention: the phrase is also reluctant self-description, warning, criticism, public verdict, and accusation. No single stance governs all 407 hits.
