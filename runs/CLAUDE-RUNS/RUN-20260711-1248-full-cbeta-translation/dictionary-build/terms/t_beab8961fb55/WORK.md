# To receive and lead (接引) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 501 occurrences in 170 texts (b033 preflight).
- Definition-formula audit found no passage that lexically defines the compound. The evidence instead shows questions about how a master 接引後學, requests to be received, descriptions of receiving later students, and a passage that explicitly tests whether the fundamental matter admits any receiving at all.
- Deployment classes retained: public-interview question and answer; named means (shout and staff); refusal of literal hand-leading; a teacher's self-description; and an explicit contrast between 接引 and 本分.
- Family cross-check: 接, 引, 接機, 應機, and 接引後學 were considered. Buddhist invocations in some allowlisted late records use 接引 for a buddha escorting beings or the dead to a pure land; those passages retain the same ordinary verbal action with a different subject and object, so item 8 does not support a second lexical sense, and they do not define the Chan institutional deployment.
- #0g result: in Chan interviews the compound names what a master does in receiving students and answering their arrival; the record also questions that category rather than supplying a fixed technique.
- Omission audit: place names such as 接引閣 and duplicated transmission witnesses were excluded. Every distinct high-value Chan deployment found above is represented by an anchor.
