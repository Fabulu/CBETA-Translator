# Mixing mud and blending water (和泥合水) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 343 occurrences in 113 texts.
- No defining X者/謂之/喚作 formula was found. A direct “what is the great road's true source?” answer and an immediately stated reciprocal formula with “where wind cannot enter” are retained without interpreting them.
- Deployment classes: member of a numbered appraisal; criticism of verbal explication; label for a teacher or speaker; direct answer; paired formula; and a contrast with distinguishing black and white at the right time.
- Family audit: 和泥, 合水, 拖泥帶水, 皂白, and 隨機逗教 were checked conceptually against the contexts. The phrase consistently names mud and water being mixed; favorable and unfavorable appraisals are different readings of the same image, not different things, so item 8 forbids splitting them.
- #0g result: Chan records repeatedly use the ordinary mixing image as an appraisal of answers, explanations, and teachers, including as a direct interview answer. The corpus does not uniformly equate it with compassionate “descending to a student's level”; that inherited gloss was rejected.
- Omission audit: five anchors preserve the unique numbered, critical, answer, reciprocal, and black/white contexts; duplicate lamp recensions were excluded.
