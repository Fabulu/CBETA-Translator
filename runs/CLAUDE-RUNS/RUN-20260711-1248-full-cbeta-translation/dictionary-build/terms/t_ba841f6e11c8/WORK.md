## Ordinary scene, family, and sense audit

- Graph composition: `乾` dry + `屎` excrement + `橛` peg/stick. Preferred target remains the concrete, intentionally unresolved “a dry shit-stick”; alternate English preserves the same literal object without choosing a material theory.
- Ordinary-scene finding: the allowlist has 11 occurrences in 11 files of the separate latrine wiping-stick term. Monastic-code passages explicitly govern using one such stick and washing after the latrine. No allowlisted occurrence equates that term with `乾屎橛`.
- Material verdict: **UNCERTAIN.** The earlier article asserted that the headword is the latrine wiping-tool or a stick of dried dung, then offered both as alternatives. The corpus establishes neither equation. Miyun's field-ridge answer cuts against presenting the latrine equation as settled but does not itself prove “turd” or dried-dung material. The entry therefore pictures only the graph-level dry excrement-associated peg/stick and reports the uncertainty.
- Chan bend: **DIRECT.** Linji and Deshan predicate the object of figures, Yunmen uses it as an answer and rebuke, and Wumen and Dahui turn Yunmen's answer into a named case and verse heading. This is where the record bends the concrete expression; no intent or symbolic doctrine is supplied.
- Sense result: **KEEP one sense.** Linji's predicate, Deshan's predicate, Yunmen's answers, the later case title, and direct questions all point to the same lexical expression. They are different deployments and competing readings, not different things. No master-specific sense is needed.
- Family retest: `雲門屎橛`, `雲門乾屎橛` (70 hits / 50 files), `乾屎橛話` (8 / 5), `屎橛` (779 / 222), `廁籌` (11 / 11), `無位真人`, `如何是佛`, and `麻三斤` were compared. The Yunmen compounds are case names; the bare shortened family remains the same object; the latrine word is a non-equated literal control; the remaining terms are cases or collocates, not senses of this headword.
- Sense-target distinguishability: no split proposed. The former WORK split between a “filthy levelling epithet” and “Yunmen's koan-answer” failed item 8 because it separated deployments/readings rather than different referents.
