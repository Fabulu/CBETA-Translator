- Allowlisted concordance: seeded spelling “raise the whisk” (竪拂), 471 occurrences in 90 texts.
- Orthographic variant: “raise the whisk” (豎拂), 2,438 occurrences. The assigned headword and ID are retained; the dominant graph variant is documented explicitly rather than silently changing the seeded SourceTerm.
- Definition-formula searches for “raising the whisk is...,” “called raising the whisk,” and “what is raising the whisk?” returned no direct definition. This absence is material: the texts record the action repeatedly but do not assign it a single gloss.
