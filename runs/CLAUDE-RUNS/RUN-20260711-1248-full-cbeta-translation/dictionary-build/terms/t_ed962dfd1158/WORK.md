## Key structural findings (describe-only)
- **The compound 四賓主 does NOT occur in the primary 臨濟錄 (T47n1985): 0 hits (main text AND
  apparatus).** There Linji names the four in prose within his long 示眾 (the section before 勘辨),
  on 主客相見. TAISHO MAIN TEXT uses 客: 喚作客看主 (0501a08) / 此是主看客 (0501a10) /
  此喚作主看主 (0501a13) / 呼為客看客 (0501a15). The 賓-graph wording (喚作賓看主…主看賓…賓看賓)
  belongs to the Ming-edition variant carried in the Taisho apparatus (foot-text note 0505003,
  次下明本有…) and to the later tradition. The numbered label 四賓主 is a later systematization
  (lamp records + 人天眼目).
- **人天眼目 (T48n2006, head 臨濟門庭)** carries the 四賓主者…也 self-definition keyed to 鼻孔:
  師家有鼻孔。名主中主。學人有鼻孔。名賓中主。師家無鼻孔。名主中賓。學人無鼻孔。名賓中賓。與曹洞賓主不同.
- **Two enumeration schemes** in corpus: 看-scheme (客-form in Taisho 臨濟錄 main text;
  賓-form in the Ming variant and later texts, e.g. X73n1457 有時主看賓，有時賓看主，有時主看主，有時賓看賓)
  and 中-scheme (主中主・賓中主・主中賓・賓中賓; 人天眼目).
- **Contrast the texts draw:** Linji's 四賓主 vs Caodong's (曹洞) — 與曹洞賓主不同; of the Caodong
  set, 四賓主。不同臨濟。主中賓。體中用也. Two distinct systems; do not conflate.
- Grouped with Linji's other numbered devices: 昔臨濟大師建立四料揀、四賓主、四照用、三玄、三要.
