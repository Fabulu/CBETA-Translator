## Retrospective public-feedback and falsification gate (2026-07-14)

- feedback-observations: Gushan Shenyan performs a prompt and immediately says ‘this is prompting you’; Hongzhi Zhengjue says each party brings up ‘this matter’; Dahui Zonggao documents both a criticized one-pass use and repeated self-directed uses across ordinary situations; Wumen Huikai contrasts picking up Zhaozhou's answer with bringing it under examination; Foyan Qingyuan thanks a late teacher for repeated prompting.
- minimal-inference: the common act is directing something into active attention, whether the agent directs another person or repeatedly recalls a saying or matter.
- ordinary-bridge: bringing something up and calling attention to it can be other-directed or self-directed without changing the identity of the act.

- verdict: licensed.
