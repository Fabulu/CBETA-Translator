# 當面錯過 — research ledger

- Corpus count: 134 hits in 67 allowlisted files.
- Definition formulas: one `所謂當面錯過` witness, included. No `X者`, `謂之`, `名為`, `喚作`, `何謂`, or `如何是` formula.
- Deployment inventory: paired verdict; warning after an assessment question; corrective warning followed by “still let me down”; warning attached to ordinary visible events; warning immediately before raising a staff; autobiographical acknowledgment in a letter.
- Variants: `當面蹉過` 158 hits in 65 files; `覿面錯過` 12/11; `眼前錯過` 3/3. Imperative collocations include “must not” 3/3, “take care not to” 7/6, and “do not” 9/8.
- Period/genre spread: addresses by several independent Ming-Qing Chinese Chan masters plus correspondence. No earlier exact-spelling witness was found in the primary Song records checked; the older corpus more often carries the close `當面蹉過` form.
- Omission audit: every distinct speech act and the formula witness are included. Variant-only KWICs were not saved because the entry's curated evidence must bear the exact headword.
