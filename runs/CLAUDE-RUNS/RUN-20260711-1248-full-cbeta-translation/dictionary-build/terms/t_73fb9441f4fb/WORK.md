# 立地 research

- Concordance: 1,209 hits / 272 allowlisted files.
- Deployment classes searched: 立地成佛 (“become a buddha on the spot”), 立地搆取 (“get it on the spot”), 立地待/聽, 立地死漢, literal standing, dozing while standing, and compounds 頂天立地 / 立地佛事.
- No formal `X者` definition found. The sitting/standing wordplay (`古人雖則道立地搆取，如今坐地還搆得也無`, verified in the corpus but not curated because a clean shorter witness was unavailable in this draft) confirms the graphs remain live.
- Included adverb anchors: immediate getting, listening right there, verdict “dead on the spot,” butcher becoming buddha on the spot. Included literal posture anchor: “do not stand here dozing.”
- Family/split audit: compared 直下, 當下, 言下, 坐地, 頂天立地, 立地成佛. Revised from a blurry single gloss to two senses because physical standing is a different referent from the adverb of immediacy. Larger idioms were not treated as independent senses of the headword.
- #0g: “on the spot” is repeatedly attached to demands and verdicts where the addressee stands; no present-moment doctrine is asserted.

## L002-B gloss-hygiene, depth, family, definition, and #0g retest

- sense-target-distinguishability: sense 0 `on the spot` names immediate occurrence or verdict; sense 1 `standing at that place` names bodily posture and location. Temporal immediacy and standing posture are different referents, not merely adverb/noun packaging.
- Definition correction: moved 三世諸佛立地聽 from immediacy to posture; the buddhas stand and listen. Added an independent immediate-awakening witness and the explicit `I now stand here watching you` posture witness. Final depth is 7 across six sources.
- Family retest: 立地成佛, 立地悟去, 直下, 當下, and 言下 support immediacy; 立地瞌睡, 立地聽, 立地看, 坐地, and 頂天立地 support physical position. Larger idioms remain related terms, not extra senses.
- #0g retest: the bend is the repeated demand or verdict occurring where one stands; the posture sense prevents that loaded deployment from being imposed on literal standing.
