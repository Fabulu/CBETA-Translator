# Outside mind, no thing stands (心外無法) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 199 hits in 100 texts.
- Formula audit: paired reciprocal formula “this thing is mind … this mind is the thing”; Shao's verse; “where could things be established?” question; later quotation and criticism.
- Deployments included: maxim, named verse, hall-address questions with a Chan-seat action, later critical comment.
- 法 audit: contextual English is required. The syntax makes 法 what could be established apart; 萬法 is rendered “the myriad things.” No bare “Dharma” is used.
- #0g deviation: the recurring phrase becomes an exchangeable Zen line: quoted in hall addresses, paired with the mountains before the eyes, and directly criticized as “two pieces.”
- Sense audit: all passages concern the same claim. Approval and criticism are different readings of one proposition, never different senses.
- Family cross-check: 心外無法, 法外無心, 萬法唯心, and 滿目青山 can be read together without assigning 法 incompatible meanings.
- Omission audit: the primary formula, named verse, institutional hall deployment, and explicit later criticism are anchored. Repeated reproductions of Shao's verse were excluded as duplicates.
