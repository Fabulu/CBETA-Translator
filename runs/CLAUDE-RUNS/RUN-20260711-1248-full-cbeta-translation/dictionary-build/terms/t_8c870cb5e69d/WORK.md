# 把定 — to hold fast

## Concordance and sense decision

- Allowlist count: **684 hits in 186 texts**.
- One corpus-wide sense: ‘grasp/take in hand’ (把) + ‘fix/settle’ (定), to hold fast. The concrete verb, the objects it takes, and the recurrent paired vocabulary ‘hold fast / release’ (把定／放行) share the same opposition. Enrichment retested whether authority-language, holding a throat, and holding heaven and earth denote different things; all are applications of the same transitive act, not new referents.

## Depth inventory

- Required formulas searched: ‘holding fast + one who’ (把定者), ‘the so-called holding fast’ (所謂把定), ‘called holding fast’ (謂之把定), ‘named holding fast’ (名為把定), ‘called holding fast’ (喚作把定), ‘what is meant by holding fast?’ (何謂把定), and ‘what is holding fast?’ (如何是把定).
- No standalone self-definition was found. A high-value equivalence formula says one eye ‘is also called the eye that holds heaven and earth fast’ (一隻眼又喚作把定乾坤眼) and ‘also named the diamond eye at the crown’ (亦名頂門具金剛眼); included in prose and as an occurrence.
- The question ‘what is the eye that holds heaven and earth fast?’ (如何是把定乾坤眼) occurs in 15 texts. Jiufeng's answer, ‘heaven and earth are inside it’ (乾坤在裏許), is included without explanatory addition.
- Text-drawn contrast: ‘when held fast, true gold loses color; when released, rubble shines’ (把定真金失色，放行瓦礫生光); the same passage calls it ‘capture and release’ (有擒有縱). Independent Xuedou witness included.
- Objects/collocations: ‘hold fast and release’ (把定放行), 15 hits / 13 texts; ‘hold heaven and earth fast’ (把定乾坤), 113 / 60; ‘hold the world fast’ (把定世界), 39 / 31; ‘hold the key crossing’ (把定要津), 29 / 22; ‘hold the throat’ (把定咽喉), 8 / 8; ‘hold firmly fast’ (牢牢把定), 6 / 6.
- Distinct deployments included: naming/equivalence; Q&A; direct contrast; objects; Wansong's inversion of holding/releasing; a formal-address parallel ‘held or not / released or not’; Hongzhi's appraisal of ‘holding the throat’ as the work of a good hand.
- Omission audit: repeated versions of the Jiufeng exchange and gold/rubble pairing were inventoried but only independent, lexicographically useful witnesses were curated. All true contrasts/equivalences are represented.

## Verification

- Ten curated KWICs; **10/10 verified**. The three additions anchor the previously prose-only collocations `把定世界`, `把定放行`, and `把定要津`; none is depth padding because each supplies a distinct object or paired formula used by the explanation.
- Wansong's commentary and Xuedou's direct record are linked. The raised Jiufeng exchange and the lineage-headed Xudeng lu passage remain unkeyed.

## Retrospective public-feedback and falsification gate (2026-07-14)

- feedback-observations: Wansong and Xuedou oppose holding fast to releasing under capture/release and killing/bringing-to-life language; Wansong explicitly places release inside holding and holding inside release; Jiufeng and Wansong name an eye that holds heaven and earth fast; Jiangshan asks whether heaven and earth are held or not; Hongzhi applies the same verb to the throat in an appraisal of an exchange.
- minimal-inference: the headword means keeping an object firmly in hand or under control, while the records make holding/releasing a recurrent paired vocabulary of exercised authority without assigning either member one invariant result.
- ordinary-bridge: grasping, securing a passage, or holding a throat are ordinary physical/control frames for preventing free movement; ‘release’ supplies the direct opposite.
- feedback-falsification-searches: exact headword; definition formulas; `把定／放行`; heaven-and-earth, world, key-crossing, throat, boundary, eye, and authority collocations; questions, answers, appraisals, rebukes, and verse; `真金／瓦礫`; related `把住`; and wider gold/silver controls.
- feedback-counterexamples: Wansong's inversion prevents equating holding with suppression and release with approval. The true-gold/rubble saying changes each object's displayed condition under holding/release but does not self-gloss gold as enlightenment. Concrete actor-object uses prevent replacing the verb with a hidden doctrinal target.
- feedback-scope: one corpus-wide transitive verb; authority and eye formulas are recurrent Chan deployments, while the true-gold/rubble evaluation remains construction-specific.
- verdict: licensed.
- feedback-inference-verdict: LICENSED — the ordinary control scene and Chan hold/release bend are directly reconstructible from the seven anchors; no external doctrine is required.
- lookup-probes: hold fast, hold firmly, keep in hand, hold in check, secure.
