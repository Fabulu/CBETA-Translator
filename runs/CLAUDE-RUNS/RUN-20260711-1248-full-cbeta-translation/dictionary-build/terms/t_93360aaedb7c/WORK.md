# Research record: join the hall (參堂)

- Corpus inventory: 529 matches in 137 files.
- Required formula search: 4/4 for the nominal “those who join the hall”; no matches for the other six definition formulas.
- Relations and variants: imperative “go to the hall” 349/99; whole-assembly imperative 7/6; “ordered him immediately to join the hall” 10/10; “then went down to the hall” 19/16.
- High-value evidence retained: Wuju's closing imperative after testing two visitors; Wuzu's immediate admission of Yuanwu; the biography in which applicants wait months while one visitor is admitted that day; Yuanwu's commentary on a monk dismissing the whole assembly; and Wenshu's imperative followed by the addressee going down to the hall.
- Sense decision: one institutional and imperative sense, “join/go to the hall.” The corpus contexts establish “join/attend” here; it is not treated as the separate verb “investigate.”
- Mechanical status: five exact headword-bearing KWICs selected; all were verified before drafting.
