## Sense and family audit

### Sense 1 — enter concentration

- Ordinary scene: someone settles into an absorbed, composed state, remains there, and may later emerge.
- Sanghanandi supplies the clearest physical timeline: seated entry, twenty-one days, emergence, then Rahulatabhadra's body/mind question.
- The reviewed unnamed woman supplies the central public case: she remains in concentration; Shakyamuni Buddha commands Manjusri to bring her out; Manjusri cannot. Huiyue Xu is the later case raiser and record-owner, not the woman or ancient-case speaker.
- Zhirong and Zhihuang debate whether entry is with or without mind and whether constant concentration can have entry or exit. Mazu, Baizhang, and Yongming criticize particular ways of taking or pursuing it. Those challenges are attested Zen deployments, not grounds for deleting the observable state.
- Search aliases: enter concentration / enter absorption / go into samadhi / become deeply concentrated / settle into a concentrated state.

### Sense 2 — enter concentration for the final time

- Different-thing finding: the Ancestor Hall Collection uses entry as a dated terminal event after transmission and immediately before the successor's biography. Vasumitra, Nagarjuna, Rahulata, and Vasubandhu occur in this repeated succession frame.
- This is not merely another reading of a temporary sitting: the collection records no later emergence, closes the life, dates the event, adds a praise, and moves to the next patriarch.
- Three notices are anchored. The sense remains **provisional** because all four measured bare succession formulas occur in one source, even though the pattern is internally repeated and structurally unambiguous.
- Search aliases make the terminal function retrievable without replacing the source's visible wording: final concentration / die in concentration / death in samadhi / enter final absorption / final absorbed state.

### Split verdict

- **SPLIT two things.** A reversible state from which Sanghanandi and the woman are expected to emerge differs from a dated succession event that ends a patriarch's biography. Temporary versus terminal is a difference in event identity, not merely in translation preference.
- sense-target-distinguishability: **KEEP both.** “Enter concentration” names a reversible state whose witnesses later emerge or are challenged to emerge. “Die in final concentration” names the dated terminal event that closes a patriarch's biography. The second target cannot describe Sanghanandi's twenty-one-day sitting, and the first alone does not tell a reader that the succession notice records a death.
- `女子入定`, `女子出定`, `全身入定`, `入正定`, `三昧`, `那伽大定`, and `出定` were checked as related families. The longer woman-case and whole-body forms preserve case or construction context; they do not buy a third sense.
