# 窠臼 research

- Concordance: 969 occurrences / 243 allowlisted texts. Searched definition formulas, falling/entering, escaping/jumping, old/current, staff-and-shout, format, and the recursive `無窠臼為窠臼` family.
- Included five distinct classes: concrete comparison, escape formula, old-versus-current contrast, format/earlier-track relation, and the unique recursive warning. Repeated late exhortations were excluded as duplicates.
- Family cross-check: 棒喝 is not itself defined as a rut; the cited question explicitly says wielding staff and shout can fail to escape a present-day rut. The definition therefore remains a fixed pattern, not a judgment on any specific implement.
- Item 8: one thing. Literal nest/mortar imagery and Chan figurative deployments do not produce two attested referents in the curated pool. Old and new ruts are instances, not senses.
- #0g: Chan bends an ordinary mould/rut into a criticism of inherited cases, standard gestures, formats, and even the formula of rejecting formulas.
