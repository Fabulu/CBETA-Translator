# Responding to things, showing a form (應物現形) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 209 hits in 105 texts.
- Definition-formula audit: the corpus repeatedly supplies “the body has no form; responding to things, it shows a form” and the comparison “like the moon in water.” It also contains direct “what is it?” public-interview questions.
- Deployment classes included: fixed comparison; direct question and concrete answer; whisk gesture; staff gesture in a hall address; list of guest-host encounter forms.
- Period/genre spread: lamp record, continuation lamp record, and collected hall-address material.
- #0g deviation: the phrase is not merely abstract description in these records. Masters ask it publicly and answer through a bed-seat, whisk, and staff—the furniture and implements of the teaching seat. This institutional fact is stated without assigning spiritual intent to the gestures.
- Sense audit: one action/referent across all evidence. Moon, furniture, and implements are deployment shapes, not separate meanings.
- Family cross-check: compatible with entries for whisk, staff, guest-host, and whole-body function; no occurrence was made to carry incompatible senses.
- Omission audit: the unique formula, comparison, question-answer form, gesture answers, and encounter-list context are represented. Repeated lamp retellings were excluded as duplicate witnesses.
