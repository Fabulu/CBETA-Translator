# Goose king (鵝王) — research inventory

- Exact allowlisted concordance: 129 hits in 79 texts. The stock formula “the goose king selects the milk” (鵝王擇乳) has 69 hits in 48 texts; “not of the duck kind” (素非鴨類) has 55 in 36.
- Definition-formula sweep: no X-is formula. The richest direct description says that milk is in water but people do not see it, and the goose king selects it. Another passage says that when water and milk are mixed, the goose king eats the milk.
- Deployment classes retained: direct description, formal hall appraisal, question about the formula's purport and its recorded answer, comparison with a lion's ability to bite, application after a raised case, and a verse that reverses the stock figure by making a goose king become a duck under one strike.
- Text-drawn relations: goose versus duck, milk versus water, and goose selection versus lion biting. These are preserved as the texts' pairings, not expanded into a theory of discernment.
- Sense test: bird, selecting action, and later appraisal all invoke the same legendary goose figure. No separate literal flock animal or title/person appeared; one corpus-wide sense.
- Family check: compared selecting milk (擇乳), water and milk (水乳), duck kind (鴨類), choosing the teaching (擇法), and possessing the eye (具眼). The entry says only what the bird is recorded doing and where masters cite it.
- Omission audit: six anchors preserve all distinct high-value classes without adding recension duplicates. The sixth is required because inversion of the goose/duck contrast is not established by any of the five affirmative deployments.
