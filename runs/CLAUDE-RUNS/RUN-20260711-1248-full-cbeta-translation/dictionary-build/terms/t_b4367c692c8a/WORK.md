# What connection does that have? (有什麼交涉) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 114 hits in 41 texts.
- Definition-formula audit: an interrogative formula rather than a noun; no “X is...” definition is expected. Direct dialogue supplies its grammatical function.
- Deployment classes included: master’s rejection of a monk’s identification; monk’s counter-question; hall criticism; case commentary rejecting a later explanation.
- Relations: the negative “no connection” form (沒交涉) and shorter “what connection?” form (有甚交涉) are family members, not new senses.
- #0g deviation: ordinary relevance language becomes a repeatable public-interview verdict on whether an answer or explanation connects with the exchange.
- Sense/family retest: speaker role and rejected object vary, but the question asks the same thing. No distinct referent or word class supports a split.
- Omission audit: dialogue, reversed speaker role, prose appraisal, and commentarial use are anchored; duplicate rebukes were excluded.
