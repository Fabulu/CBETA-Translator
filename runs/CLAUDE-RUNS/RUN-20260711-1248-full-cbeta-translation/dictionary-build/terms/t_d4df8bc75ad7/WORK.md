# 銅頭鐵額 research

- Concordance: 208 hits / 114 texts.
- Deployment classes anchored: patch-robed-monk epithet, answer to “What is buddha?”, Deshan staff verse, mountain collective, and entering-room staff verse.
- Formula searches did not yield a direct lexical definition. Recurrent `銅頭鐵額漢` confirms the human referent.
- Sense test: adjective and substantivized epithet name the same kind of person. No second literal metal-object sense was found.
- Family cross-check: 衲僧, 棒, and 鐵漢 agree with the formidable-person target; staff passages also show the epithet does not mean invulnerable.
- #0g: an old martial/monstrous epithet becomes a stock Chan label for a formidable respondent and is repeatedly tested under the staff.
