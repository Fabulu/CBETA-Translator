## Sense decision

One sense remains. The evidence names one activity/category, even when different speakers prescribe it, define its graphs, reject it as a hall predicate, criticize a deficient performance, or deny that dhyana is reducible to bodily sitting. Those are different claims about the same thing, not different referents. No noun/verb grammar split was created.

Preferred target: **sitting dhyana**. Reader-facing aliases include ordinary search probes plus retrieval-only `zazen` and `zuochan`; neither transliteration is presented as a separate object.

## Independent-review correction — split incompatible referents

- Reviewer challenge: the one-sense entry merged a regulated cross-legged body/breath procedure with an explicit non-postural redefinition, even though `禪非坐臥` and `坐禪終日臥` contradict bodily sitting as a necessary predicate.
- Re-adjudication: SPLIT. Sense one is the regulated seated procedure, anchored by Changlu Zongze's sequential manual, Dehui's hall orientation, Guting Shanjian's prolonged upright sitting, and five direct critiques/rejections of that recognizable activity. Sense two is non-postural sitting dhyana, anchored by Huineng's positive graph-by-graph definition, Nanyue Huairang's “dhyana is not sitting or lying,” and Fu Dashi's “sitting dhyana, lying down all day.”
- Different-things test: quiet place, mat, cross-legged legs, body, breath, inward hall orientation, and upright duration are compatible predicates of a seated procedure. Not sitting or lying and lying down all day are incompatible with that bodily referent; Huineng supplies a different positive referent based on thought and inwardly seeing nature unmoved.
- Revised feedback-inference-verdict: KEEP TWO — the split is earned by incompatible predicates and independent positive definition, not by approval, grammar, or outside doctrine.
- Revised feedback-counterexamples: Linji, Baozhi, Zhaoqing Shengdeng, Zhanran Yuancheng, and the tile case criticize the bodily procedure but remain in sense one because criticism does not change its object. Only explicit non-postural uses enter sense two.
- Revised feedback-scope: one corpus-wide regulated activity plus one corpus-wide non-postural redefinition; neither is presented as the sole meaning of every occurrence.
- Revised lookup-probes: sitting dhyana; seated dhyana; regulated sitting; dhyana procedure; non-postural sitting dhyana; dhyana beyond posture; dhyana not sitting or lying.
- Revised independent-falsification-verdict: PASS AFTER SPLIT. Nine anchors support the seated procedure and three independent anchors support the non-postural referent.
- sense-target-distinguishability: KEEP TWO — `the seated dhyana procedure` names a regulated bodily activity, while `non-postural sitting dhyana` names the explicitly defined referent for which bodily sitting is not necessary. The targets remain distinguishable without their explanations.
