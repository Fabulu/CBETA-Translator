## Sense analysis
One sense only. Everywhere in the corpus 麻三斤 = **Dongshan Shouchu's answer to 如何是佛**, cited verbatim. Later masters do not *bend* the meaning; they raise it, versify it, and probe it. So: a single corpus-wide sense with a clear origin (not a corpus-wide vs. master-specific split like 水牯牛).
