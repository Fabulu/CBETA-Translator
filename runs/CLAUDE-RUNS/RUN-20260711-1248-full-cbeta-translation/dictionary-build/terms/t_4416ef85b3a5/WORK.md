## Retrospective public-feedback and falsification gate
- feedback-observations: “The myriad forms arrayed” preserves the graphs but leaves an English reader asking what belongs to the array. The retained contexts answer that question through landscapes, moonlight, ordinary and holy beings, and the full field addressed by a speaker.
- minimal-inference: The converging members and predicates license “the whole array of phenomena.” They do not license replacing the collective with enlightenment, mind, reality, or another hidden substance.
- ordinary-bridge: English uses “all phenomena,” “the whole array of things,” or “all forms” for a totalized multiplicity presented together.
- feedback-falsification-searches: Rechecked headword and reversed-order counts; `森羅萬象是`, `森羅萬象從`, `森羅萬象皆`, `森羅萬象一`, and `森羅萬象盡` frames; first/second-moon exchange; scenery, location, limit, arising, illness, medicine, and question deployments. Tested for a named title/person and for a referent distinct from the collective; none emerged.
- feedback-counterexamples: Fayan Wenyi's first/second-moon pair shows that headword order can switch without producing a different lexical thing. Kongsou Yin's medicine predicate and Shangfeng Bencai's illness predicate oppose each other, so neither predicate defines the collective.
- feedback-scope: The gloss names the collectively presented multiplicity. Local claims that it is medicine, ill, within empty space, or produced from a given source remain speaker-specific predicates.
- verdict: KEEP one sense, revise the preferred target to a natural totalizing collective, and add Hongzhi Zhengjue's arising/arraying witness for the 500–1,999-hit depth floor and deployment spread.
- feedback-inference-verdict: ACCEPT “the whole array of phenomena” from converging membership and grammar; REJECT a hidden metaphysical identity.
- lookup-probes: `all phenomena`; `myriad phenomena`; `ten thousand things`; `all forms`; `whole array of things`.
- opening-interpretation-verdict: PASS after revision. The first sentence now says what the collective gathers.
- plain-english-image-verdict: PASS. A reader can picture many forms or appearances spread together across a scene or world.
- nested-compound-verdict: PASS. Reverse-order `萬象森羅` is an ordering variant; `萬象`, `森羅`, and `山河大地` remain independently related expressions and do not donate extra senses.
- verb-frame-verdict: PASS. Reaching a limit, standing within, becoming arrayed, being called ill or medicinal, and interpenetrating all predicate one collective field; the opposed predicates are local claims, not incompatible objects.
- family-definition-retest: PASS against `萬象森羅`, `萬象`, `森羅`, and `山河大地`. The headword is the combined totalizing collective; the related entries remain separately searchable.
- sense-target-distinguishability: KEEP one sense. Different members, word order, and speaker predicates do not create different things.
- omission-audit: Seven anchors cover paired answer, limit comparison, illness chain, moon location, illness/medicine contrast, scenery gathering, and arising/arraying across five source texts.
