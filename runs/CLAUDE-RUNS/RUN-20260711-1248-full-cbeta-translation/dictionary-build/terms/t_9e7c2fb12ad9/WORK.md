# Cat (猫兒) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 513 occurrences in 97 texts. 猫兒 is the graph variant of 貓兒, independently common enough to require a searchable entry.
- Sense audit: the case animal, living cats used on the teaching seat, mouse-catching cats, and proverbial cats are the same kind of thing. Different readings and case force do not license a split.
- Distinct deployments retained: early lamp case; later case commentary explicitly debating killing guilt and a textual variant; ordinary mouse-catching animal; a living cat lifted before the assembly.
- Precept cross-check: the *Book of Serenity* reports that one critic reproached Nanquan's group for “killing living beings and creating guilt,” while a defender claimed an old text described only a simulated cutting gesture. This is an attested controversy, not an entry-level verdict on what happened.
- Family comparison: same definition as 貓兒; compatible with 南泉斬猫, 斬猫 and 兩堂爭猫. Variant difference is orthographic only.
- Omission audit: historical case, later controversy, ordinary use, and live teaching-seat deployment are represented.
