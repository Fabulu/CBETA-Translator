# 婆子燒庵 — research

- Concordance: 75 hits in 34 allowlisted texts.
- Definition-formula search: no lexical formula; numerous texts narrate the case itself. Full witnesses checked in J26nB178, X71n1418, and X72n1437.
- Deployment inventory: case title; full retelling; public question about later support for the monk; prose appraisal of the woman; contrary appraisal proposing the monk laugh; verse and case-collection headings.
- Depth decision: four headword-bearing anchors cover raising, questioning, prose comment, and verse. The complete story is summarized from full allowlist witnesses and represented in SourceTexts.
- Family cross-check: 枯木倚寒巖 and 三冬無暖氣 are the response inside the case; neither supplies a second referent for 婆子燒庵. 庵/菴 is orthographic variation.
- Sense audit: every hit refers to the same named encounter or its title. Later favorable and unfavorable readings are appraisals of one thing, not separate senses.
- #0g: an ordinary burning becomes the durable label for an encounter that later masters publicly question and comment on. No sexual, psychological, or doctrinal explanation is imported.
- Omission audit: later interpretations were sampled only where their Chinese wording is explicit; repetitive verse titles were excluded.
