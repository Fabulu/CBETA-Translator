## Sense analysis — ONE corpus-wide sense
"The great death" = utter dying of the discriminating self, the gate that MUST be followed by revival — never a terminus. Two idiomatic forms of the one metaphor:
- **大死一番** — imperative "die the great death once (then come alive)": 大死一番方堪湊泊; 大死一番，不怕不活; paired with 絕後再甦 (revive after being utterly cut off).
- **大死底人** — "the person who has died the great death," the Zhaozhou–Touzi koan: 大死底人却活時如何 → 不許夜行。投明須到.
