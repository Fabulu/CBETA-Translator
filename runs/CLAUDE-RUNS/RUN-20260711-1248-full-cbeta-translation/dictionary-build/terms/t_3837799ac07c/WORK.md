## Sense and family audit

- One corpus-wide figure referent. Accepting/refusing, washing/not washing, residence, and gourd are predicates or episodes of the same Xu You, not different things.
- Chaofu, Mount Ji, ear-washing, ox-watering, and the gourd remain related allusion elements. Xu You is a pre-Zen figure invoked by masters and therefore belongs here under #0g, not on the lineage master roster.
- #0g deviation: the classical recluse is not merely praised; masters turn him into public answers, map him onto host-and-guest positions, negate his famous action, and call it a display of lofty purity.
