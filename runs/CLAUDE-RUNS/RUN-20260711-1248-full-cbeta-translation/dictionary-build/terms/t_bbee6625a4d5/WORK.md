# On the lump of red flesh (赤肉團上) — research inventory

- Concordance: 316 occurrences in 144 allowlisted texts; broader “lump of red flesh” (赤肉團), 441.
- Definition search: no direct lexical definition. One “what is on the lump of red flesh...?” question belongs to the Nanyuan “wall stands a thousand fathoms” exchange and was included as a deployment, not promoted into a separate sense.
- Two principal case families harvested: Linji's “true person of no rank” statement and Nanyuan Huiyong's “wall stands a thousand fathoms” statement with the overturning of the Chan seat.
- Variants: “beside the lump of red flesh” (赤肉團邊), 36; exact “inside the lump of red flesh” (赤肉團裏), 0. The latter is explicitly excluded.
- Deployment range: address to an assembly, quoted saying, case question, later comment, and Dahui's recombination with the present assembly.
- Attribution audit: Linji's own record and section witnesses link to Linji; Nanyuan witnesses remain null; Dahui's own address links to Dahui.
- Omission audit: both independent case families, the principal collocation, and verified positional variants are represented.
