# 問話 research

- Count: 871 hits / 210 allowlisted texts; 問話者 274/124; 問話僧 52/36.
- Definition/self-comment search: `誡問話` supplies the richest direct account, pairing questioning with putting forward a question, presenting doubt, and requesting instruction, and contrasting this with congratulatory seasonal filler.
- Deployment inventory: public invitation; capable-questioner challenge; multiple monks competing to question; questioner noun; refusal/thirty-blows case; explicit criticism of bad questioning. Five anchors cover the unique claims; the questioner noun is covered in prose and two anchors.
- Family cross-check: `僧問` is a recurrent narrative formula, `問答` names the exchange pair, and `問話` focuses on putting the question and the questioner. `上堂`, `普說`, and `室中` are settings/formats in which it occurs. Definition retained after ensuring no format was collapsed into the act.
- Omission audit: many routine instances duplicate the same public invitation; later Deshan repetitions were excluded after one early commentary witness anchored the distinct case-level use.
- #0f.8 thing audit: **one sense retained.** Verb uses (“come out and put a question”), agent compounds (`問話者`, “questioner”; `問話僧`, “questioning monk”), and retrospective phrases (`問話底僧`, “the monk who questioned”) all point to the same questioning turn, with ordinary derivational changes rather than different objects. Apparent `所問話` hits segment as `所問` + `話` (“whatever was asked” + “saying”) and do not establish a second lexical object. No title/person or genuinely master-specific referent was found.
