- Concordance: 10,572 occurrences in 168 allowlisted texts.
- Formula search: 12 apparent “Dharma heir is/one who...” hits in 10 texts; 1 “called Dharma heir” question. Most are grammatical person-labels, not definitions.

- Structural caution: headings establish a lineage grouping but do not assign every later quoted exchange to the heading master.
- Omission audit: all distinct syntactic and catalogue uses found are included.

## d001-A depth repair (2026-07-13)

- Enrichment exposed an item-8 split: 法嗣 denotes both a person (lineage heir) and the succession relation/status (lineage affiliation).
- Preserved all five old anchors, moving 未詳法嗣者 to the relation sense, and added six verified anchors: direct “whose heir?”, received succession, no heir, lay heir, collateral count, and succession document.
- Final depth: 2 senses, 11 occurrences: person-role 8; relation/status 3.
- Family check: 嗣法 is the separate verb “inherit the lineage.” The recurrent 師今得法嗣何人 cannot be glossed as “obtain lineage heirs.”
- Additional numerical lineage headings were excluded as duplicates of the represented counted-heading class.
