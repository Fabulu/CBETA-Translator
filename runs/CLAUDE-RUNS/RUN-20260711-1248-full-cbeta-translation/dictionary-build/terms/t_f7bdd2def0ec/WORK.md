## Sense split and historical finding

- sense-target-distinguishability: TWO SENSES — “the phrase that cuts off the many streams” is the named middle constituent of a transmitted three-member set; “cut off all the streams” is a freely predicated action. A named constituent and an action are different referents, not noun/verb packaging of one referent.
- The Eye of Humans and Gods explicitly says Deshan Yuanming later separated Yunmen Wenyan's words into three phrases. Deshan states the set in first person in his own Compendium of the Five Lamps section. Jiuding Huiquan later calls the transmitted set Yunmen's three phrases. The entry preserves all three facts.
- The historical association does not create a master-specific lexical sense; both senses remain corpus-wide with null SenseKey and null sense MasterName.
- Falsification check: if the purported free verbal witnesses were only ellipses for the named middle phrase, the second sense would collapse. Yuanwu Keqin and Hongzhi Zhengjue use the expression predicatively with action and working complements, so it stands.
