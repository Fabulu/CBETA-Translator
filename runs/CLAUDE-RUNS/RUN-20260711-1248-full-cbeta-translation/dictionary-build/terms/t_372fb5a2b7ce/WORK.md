# Living phrase (活句) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 412 occurrences in 128 texts.
- Definition-formula searches: “a living phrase is...” (活句者), 3; “the so-called living phrase” (所謂活句), 1; “called a living phrase” (謂之活句), 2; “named a living phrase” (名為活句), 8; “what is meant by a living phrase?” (何謂活句), 1; “what is a living phrase?” (如何是活句), 45 in 35 texts.
- Direct definitions included: “speech within speech is called a dead phrase; speech without speech is called a living phrase”; the later eight-part definition excluding verbal explanation, a reasoning track, thought, and deliberation; and Gulin’s description that a net will not hold it and a call will not turn its head.
- Deployment range included: technical noun; explicit definition; contrast with dead phrase; instruction to investigate one and not the other; direct question-answer; later reversal; and the three-member series living/dead/neither-dead-nor-living.
- Collocations: “investigate the living phrase” (參活句) 90; “under the living phrase” (活句下) 33; “dead phrase” (死句) 372; “neither-dead-nor-living phrase” (不死不活句) 18; exact “living phrase, dead phrase” sequence (活句死句) 3.
- Text-drawn complication included: Gulin says “a dead phrase is precisely a living phrase; a living phrase is precisely a dead phrase.” The entry therefore presents classifications as each text states them rather than supplying a universal test.
- Exclusions: one “living phrase is...” hit is merely “one who investigates the living phrase” (參活句者), not a definition. A late “so-called living phrase and living wording” discussion of Zhuangzi is not used to define the Chan corpus-wide term. The direct eight-part definition is included in prose but not curated as an occurrence because its exact source uses nonstandard middle-dot punctuation that was not safely reproduced in the draft KWIC.
- Attribution audit: Dongshan Shouchu and Yuanwu Keqin are exact roster values. Quoted/late section material with uncertain roster correspondence remains null.
- Omission audit: every distinct self-definition, contrast, reversal, deployment type, and morphological variant found is included or excluded with a reason.
