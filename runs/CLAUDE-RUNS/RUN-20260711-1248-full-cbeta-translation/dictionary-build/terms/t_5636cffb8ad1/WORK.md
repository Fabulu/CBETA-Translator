# 擬對 research

- Concordance: 406 hits in 96 allowlisted texts.
- No lexical self-definition found. The corpus repeatedly defines the event structurally: prompt → `擬對` (“make to answer”) → interruption.
- Deployment classes retained: departure plus verdict; rebuke identifying hesitation; strike followed by narrated awakening; covered mouth; full prompt/response sequence. The last two overlap historically but preserve distinct textual witnesses and one supplies the full question.
- Family cross-check: compared `擬議` (deliberate/make to discuss), `下語` (offer a comment), `對機` (answer the occasion), and `問答`. `擬對` is narrower: the answer is only impending or attempted.
- Sense test: one verb in every sampled deployment. Different interruptions and outcomes are not different things.
- #0g/public-interview gate: its distinctive Chan load is narratological and public—the term marks the instant an answer is demanded but not completed.
