# As I knew (情知) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 371 occurrences in 147 texts (current research/preflight variance from the earlier wave estimate is recorded rather than silently normalized).
- Formula search yields no lexical self-definition. The recurring syntax is 情知 + person + predicate, and standalone 情知 as a reply.
- Deployment classes retained: a master's anticipatory appraisal of a monk's inability, recognition that someone has met an adept, later commentary anticipating a mistaken reading, a student's standalone acknowledgment, and a teacher's confirmation of a stock answer.
- Family/sense audit: 情知汝, 情知你, and standalone 情知 all denote recognition or expected knowledge, not different objects. “Feeling-knowing” would turn graph components into an unattested technical faculty; item 8 supports one adverbial/verbal sense only.
- #0g result: the ordinary idiom is heavily deployed in public interviews as a speaker's immediate “I knew it” assessment of the other turn. The entry states that observable placement without assigning spiritual force.
- Omission audit: duplicate lamp recensions of the same early exchanges were not multiplied. The five anchors represent distinct speakers, genres, and conversational shapes.
