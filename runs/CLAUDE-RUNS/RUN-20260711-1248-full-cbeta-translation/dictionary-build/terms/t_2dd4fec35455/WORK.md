# Dead ashes (死灰) — research inventory

- Allowlisted concordance: 70 hits in 52 texts.
- Definition-formula sweep: no stable “dead ashes are…” definition. The corpus instead supplies predicates and contrasts: a mind “like dead ashes,” emptying the mind “like drenched dead ashes” criticized as useless, and flames or a bean issuing from dead ashes.
- Distinct deployments retained: description/instruction, explicit criticism, reversal in the cold-stove image, and a formal taking-up-the-whisk address using fire from dead ashes.
- Family check: compared cold ashes (寒灰), dead wood (枯木), cold stove (寒爐), a bean bursting in cold ashes (冷灰豆爆), stagnant water (死水), and guarding a corpse (守屍鬼). The target remains the physical ash image, not the interpretive roadmap gloss “extinguished, lifeless state.”
- Sense test: the allowlisted occurrences use one ash image in similes and figurative predicates; no independent occurrence naming a different literal object was found. Approval, criticism, and rekindling are different textual appraisals of the same image, never separate senses.
- Omission audit: four anchors preserve each high-value deployment class and independent genre spread; additional hits repeat the dead-ash/dead-wood pairing or its rekindling.
