## Sense-split and scope adjudication

- One corpus-wide sense retained. `宗門中事`, `宗門下`, and collection-title modifiers all refer to the inherited Zen lineage and its matters or records; they do not name different things.
- Noun use in a title is grammar, not polysemy. No master-specific sense was established.
- Falsification test: a stable concrete-door use, a separately attested metaphysical entity, or a title use no longer referring to the lineage would require a split. None was found in the audited families.
- Counterexample control: Yuanwu's source-and-mind explanation does not license glossing every occurrence as metaphysical “source”; the broad deployment remains institutional.
