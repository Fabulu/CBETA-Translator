BENDS THE WORD away from ordinary-Chinese / Buddhist usage — an *attestable*
cultural/institutional fact, not projected mysticism?

Counts: **PASS 14 · MISS 3 · NONE 1** (18 terms).

| 拂子 | **MISS** | Entry catalogs gestures (raise/strike/throw) but *explicitly disclaims* "hidden symbolic meaning" and never states the institutional deviation the guide itself names: the whisk is **the master's teaching-seat implement, the emblem of teaching authority** — raised in the high seat as the answer to "what do you use to show people," which is why "all the sages are on the whisk-tip." Add it. Attest: `百丈豎起拂子`; `拂子擊禪床` (288×); `從上許多賢聖，緫在山僧拂子頭上`. |
