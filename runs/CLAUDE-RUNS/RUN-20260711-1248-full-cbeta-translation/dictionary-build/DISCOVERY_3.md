# DISCOVERY_3 — Folk proverbs & stock four-character sayings Zen repurposes

Category (#0g flyswatter test): folk sayings / classical 成語 that ordinary Chinese uses plainly, but which the
Chan records LOAD with a teaching verdict (usually a rebuke: futile effort, mistaken identity, belated action,
blind imitation, working the impossible). All candidates below are:

- **NOT already in `termbase.v2.json`** (checked against all 263 SourceTerms, 2026-07-12)
- **Zen-scoped** counts via `zc.count` (462-text allowlist), freq ≥ 12 hits
- **Confirmed** by reading an attesting passage (verbatim, allowlist file)

Ranked by a blend of frequency, purity of the folk-proverb category, and strength/clarity of the Zen deviation.

## Runners-up (verified, lower purity or overlaps existing families)
- **效顰 / 東施效顰** (117/66 · 1/1) "imitate the frown" (Zhuangzi) — blind imitation; strong but 東施 form near-zero, so headword = 效顰.
- **拋磚引玉** variant **拋塼引玉** (23/17) — same idiom, 塼 orthography; fold into #19.
- **賊過張弓** (34/27) — shorter variant of #5; fold in as collocation.
- **抱橋柱** (77/42) "hugging the bridge-pillar (to bathe)" — clinging to a fixed support; folk-ish, verify sense split before authoring.
- **買帽相頭** (68/42) "buy the hat to fit the head" — tailoring the teaching to the man; borderline Zen-native vs folk.
- **繫驢橛** (227/117) "the peg you tie the donkey to" — a fixation/hitching-post metaphor; high freq but more Chan-native image than folk proverb.

## Excluded as OUT of category (generic-Buddhist unreality images, not folk proverbs)
空花/空華, 龜毛/兔角/龜毛兔角, 石女, 木人, 木馬, bare 泥牛, 盲龜(浮木), 火裏蓮 — these are Buddhist emblems of
unreality, not ordinary-Chinese sayings Zen repurposes; they belong to a different discovery bucket.

## Method note
Brainstormed classical 成語 + Chan proverb-shapes, batch-counted with `zc.count` (allowlist-scoped), then
pulled a verbatim attesting window with `zc.find` on the top-hit file for each finalist. Saying-marker mining
(諺云/常言道/古人道/俗云…) was run but mostly fronts famous Chan *verses*, not folk proverbs, so the ranked list
is brainstorm-driven and count-verified. All 26 confirmed absent from the current termbase.
