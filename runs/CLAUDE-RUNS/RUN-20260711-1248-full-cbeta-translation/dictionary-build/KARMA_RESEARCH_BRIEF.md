## Three attested registers that must be represented or expressly rejected

The preaching/exhortation register contains direct assertions: `永斷輪迴`; `六道輪迴，無有休息`; `茫茫業海`;
past good/evil as cause and present pain/pleasure as effect; `入地獄如箭`; `定業難逃`; and `因果歷然`.

An apophatic awakened-person register is also direct evidence, not a position to erase. `少室六門`
(T48n2009 at 0374a17–20; traditionally attributed to Bodhidharma but commonly dated later) states that talk of cause
and effect is an outsider teaching, calls the buddha “a person without karma, without cause and effect”
(`佛是無業人，無因果`), and continues `無因無果` and `佛是無作人`. Qianshan Shengren likewise
places “no cause and no effect” in a chain that also negates buddha, nirvana, attainment, and beings to save. Preserve
the attribution caveat; do not silently turn the traditional attribution into secure authorship.

The corpus glosses this negating language with its own category. `禪源諸詮集都序` and `宗鏡錄` place
`無因無果` beside “not produced and not extinguished” and “neither ordinary nor sage,” then say `皆是遮詮，遣非蕩跡`
— all are apophatic predications that sweep away traces. This `遮詮` self-gloss is mandatory evidence for how the
records classify the negation; it does not license a free-standing modern theory.

The third register condemns turning denial into a thesis. `撥無因果` (97 hits / 71 texts) is a named error. One
especially relevant passage warns that readers
take the ancients' rhetorical flourishes and situation-adapted talk and thereby deny cause and effect.

The fox case is also ironized across independent masters. Preserve both sides rather than selecting a doctrinal
winner: Wumen calls `不落` and `不昧` “two throws, one game” and recasts five hundred lives as a win; Chushi Fanqi,
Minshu, and Dahui likewise refuse to let `不昧因果` settle the public-interview question. This does not by itself
prove that the corpus denies karmic causation.

## 無繩自縛 control

The phrase is a real, widespread reproach for self-created conceptual entanglement: fixation on wording, two sides,
or a teacher's question. Only 2 of 257 hits (0.8%) were found within ±60 characters of karma-family vocabulary, so it
is not established as a karma phrase. The J34nB300 passage is mandatory counterevidence: it rebukes sweeping away
`不落` and clinging to `不昧` as “binding oneself without a rope,” yet continues by affirming `不昧因果`. Refusal of
the dilemma and affirmation of causation occur together.

whether these are different senses at all: the referent of 業 or 因果 may remain constant while the textual stance changes,
