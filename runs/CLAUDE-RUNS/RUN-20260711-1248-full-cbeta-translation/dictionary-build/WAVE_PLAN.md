# Zen Dictionary - Wave Roadmap (b002 to b036)

**Total head terms:** 517, grouped into **35 waves** of ~15, ranked highest-value first.

**Already-done terms (23) excluded per brief:** 水牯牛, 異類中行, 本來面目, 平常心, 即心是佛, 佛性, 話頭, 祖師西來意, 露地白牛, 見性, 家風, 五位, 棒喝, 賓主, 機鋒, 末後句, 僧問, 凡情聖見, 和尚, 回互, 如何是佛, 序, 金鎖玄路.

## Selection basis
- **Curated from Chan expertise, validated by corpus frequency.** Every candidate was counted against a tag-stripped concatenation of **all 462 texts in `zen-corpus.json`** (the Zen allowlist only, never the full CBETA canon), assembled from `C:\temp\NewTranslationrepos\CbetaZenTexts\xml-p5`.
- **Ranking = value tier (centrality) first, then corpus frequency.** This is deliberately NOT a raw frequency dump: generic-but-frequent function words were excluded, and rare-but-central technical terms (e.g. 四照用 ct.32, 三玄三要 ct.458) are ranked high on merit. Frequency confirmed each term genuinely recurs across the corpus and broke ties within a tier.
- **Numbers in parentheses = raw occurrence count** across the 462-text allowlist (substring match on tag-stripped text). Treat as a recurrence signal / upper bound, not a concordance - some counts include incidental sub-string hits.
- **Two headwords were corrected against the corpus:** Yunmen's first phrase is written **函蓋乾坤** (158), not 涵蓋乾坤 (1); his three-word teaching is **顧鑒咦**, with the 顧鑑咦 variant noted. This is exactly the primary-source check the guide (Sec.5) demands.

## Quality-tail caveat (honest)
The dictionary-worthy Zen vocabulary does **not** thin out before 500 - the corpus supports well over 517 genuine head terms. The **last ~4 waves (b033-b036)** are deliberately lower-value: narrative/gesture formulae (下座, 卓拄杖, 撫掌), critiques of stillness or fixation, and a few verbatim-rare idioms. **8 terms fall below 12 raw occurrences** and are flagged `[thin]` below - all genuinely Chan but appearing mostly inside longer phrases or with character variants (困來即眠, 沈空滯寂, 一絲不掛, 一日不作一日不食, 眉毛墮落, 蒸砂作飯, 閑葛藤, 斬葛藤). Keep them, but expect their entries to lean on a single canonical locus rather than broad multi-source spread. Beyond b036 the next tier (not listed) is regional/master-specific hapax and monastic-office vocabulary - author those on demand, not in bulk.

## Families & Sec.5b nesting flags (so waves do not split a family awkwardly)

These groups are ONE lexical family and should be authored together / cross-linked in `RelatedTerms`, even where value-ranking scattered them across waves. Wave numbers in brackets.

- **Caodong Five Ranks** (all b005): 正中偏 / 偏中正 / 正中來 / 兼中至 / 兼中到, with emblem 君臣. Each rank is meaningless without the others - author as one set.
- **Linji's 'Four-' sets**: 四料揀 [b006] expands into 奪人不奪境 / 奪境不奪人 / 人境俱奪 / 人境俱不奪 [b025]; 四賓主 [b005] into 主中賓 / 賓中主 [b025]; 四照用 [b006] pairs with 照用 [b008]; 三玄三要 [b004]. Keep each 'four' adjacent to its members when authoring, though tiers separate them.
- **Linji's Four Shouts**: 金剛王寶劍 / 踞地師子 / 探竿影草 [b025-b026] + the fourth (fold into 一喝 [b006]).
