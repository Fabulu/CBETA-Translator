# Cohorts 1–3 genuine full-case decisions — entries 36–45

## 36. 鐵牛之機 (`t_df2096b961c1`)

All six complete units support the inherited “mechanism of the iron ox” formula, explicitly tied
to the ancestral seal and the alternatives moving, staying, or neither. Mian Xianjie, Lingyan
Chongque, and Yuanwu Keqin directly deploy it. O4 is Tian'an Sheng raising Fengxue Yanzhao's case,
not Fengxue speaking locally. O5 is an unnamed questioner quoting the case. O6 is Yuanwu's Blue
Cliff case introduction, again with Fengxue as quoted source rather than stored utterer.

## 37. 向上事 (`t_e84753568cda`)

All ten units support “the matter beyond / the upward matter,” normally posed as a public question
or qualification. O6 and O9 are Yunmen Wenyan raising earlier Dongshan and Xuefeng exchanges, so
Yunmen is the local utterer. O7 belongs to the explicitly headed Longjun Mountain master, not
Zhaozhou; he is retained as named-unrostered. Named and unnamed questioners remain distinct from
the masters who answer them.

## 38. 梵王 (`t_088731c7824b`)

All six complete units support “Brahma king,” not a Chan master-name or a metaphorical sense.
The actors differ sharply: a merchant asks whether the Buddha is one; Brahma King Equal Conduct
asks the Buddha a doctrinal question; Baizhang Zhiying directly says that a Brahma king leads;
two unnamed monks use the figure in public questions; and an unnamed Chengtian record-owner uses
it in an answer. The prior compiler labels on O2 and O3 were false. No new sense is warranted.

## 39. 草賊 (`t_09909bd0c29e`)

The six full cases retain the encounter-verdict sense “petty bandit.” O1 is the unnamed monk's
answer to Linji, not Linji's speech. O2 belongs to Chongfan Yu; the inherited Tiantong Danjiao
name was leaked from the preceding section. Linji directly utters O3, and Zhaozhou directly
utters O4–O6 in three records of the hermit inspection. The explanation's stale Tiantong name
was corrected to Chongfan Yu; the definition otherwise holds.

## 40. 白椎 (`t_0ecac939496b`)

All seven units confirm the formal mallet proclamation and specifically disprove the tempting
“white-colored mallet” reading. The actors are not interchangeable: Su Shi authors O1's appeal;
O2 is a contents heading; the unnamed hall coordinator proclaims O3; O4 and O7 are compiler
completion markers; O5 is a preface author's first-person description of Juelang; and O6 is the
compiler naming Zigu as ritual performer. No occurrence justifies putting the described master in
`MasterName` unless he actually utters the headword. The existing definition holds.

## 41. 一炷香 (`t_0f4c2ed08d86`)

All six full cases retain the counted ceremonial object “one stick of incense.” O1 cannot be
Linji: its first-person speaker explicitly dedicates the incense to “my late teacher Linji” and
describes encounters with Sansheng and Dajue. O2–O5 remain direct ceremonial speech by Shanci Ji,
Yulin Tongxiu, Shiqi Tongyun, and Huanyou Zhengchuan. O6 is direct memorial speech by Faxi Yin,
not compiler narration. The explanation's false claim that Linji dedicated incense to his own
teacher was corrected.

## 42. 棒喝 (`t_0f97bfab265c`)

Ten full cases retain one paired action-set, “stick-blows and shouts.” O1 and O4 belong to unnamed
monks questioning Yulin and Fushi, not to the respondents. O2 is third-person narration about
Linji, not securely Baichi's direct speech. O8 is layman Yu Jisheng's question to Wuyi. O10 is
Tian'an Sheng voicing a comic Indra scene in his own evening address. The inherited direct
speaker assignments on O3, O5–O7, and O9 survive. The definition remains supported.

## 43. 東山水上行 (`t_114bbd284d1c`)

Eight complete cases retain the fixed Yunmen saying and its public afterlife without inventing a
decoding. Yunmen directly answers O1. O2 is Yuanwu Keqin locally raising Yunmen and contrasting
his own answer, so Yunmen moves to quoted source. O3–O4 are unnamed questioners asking the
saying's import. Tianran authors O5's verse; Juebao raises and versifies it in O6–O7; Dahui
recounts his encounter with Yuanwu in O8. The definition and explicit refusal to decode hold.

## 44. 臨濟喝 (`t_1403ddf1e83b`)

Seven complete cases retain “Linji's shout” as a named lineage device used, compared, requested,
and criticized. Huangbo Sheng and Dahui directly use it in O1–O2 and O6. O3–O5 are questions by
unnamed monks, not speech by the responding masters. O7 is Xiangyan Zhixian explicitly asking
Sansheng whether he brought Linji's shout. The inherited actor decisions survive full reading;
the definition's inclusion of later criticism is supported.

## 45. 分別 (`t_15026800437e`)

Nine full units retain the act of distinguishing or discrimination as one referent across
quotation, direct address, doctrinal exposition, and biography. Huineng, Dahui, Shiyu, Yongming,
and Huangbo directly utter O1–O5. O6 is quoted scripture narration, not the awakening master's
speech. O7 is an unnamed record-owner's opening-fire address. Faming of Xingzhou Kaiyuan utters
O8's death verse. Yongjia, not Huineng, says O9's “you yourself give rise to discrimination.”
The definition remains supported after the speaker corrections.
