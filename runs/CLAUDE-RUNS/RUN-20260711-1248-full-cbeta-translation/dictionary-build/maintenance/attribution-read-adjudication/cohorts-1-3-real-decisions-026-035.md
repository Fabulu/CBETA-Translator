# Cohorts 1–3 genuine full-case decisions — entries 26–35

## 26. 法衣 (`t_850d52f97185`)

All six complete structural units support two distinguishable but related uses: the Dharma robe
as the robe handled or transmitted inside the institution, and a robe formally bestowed by an
official or patron. The split survives; neither use changes merely because a different person
handles or gives the same kind of robe.

- S1/O1: compiler stage direction for Wuzu Fayan lifting the robe; Wuzu's quoted words begin
  after `云`.
- S1/O2: compiler biography narrates Yangqi Fanghui taking and displaying the robe on the day
  he accepts the invitation; Yangqi's direct speech follows `曰`.
- S1/O3–O4: two biographical versions narrate Houfeng sending Longji Youyun Zongmou a Dharma
  robe, bamboo tally, and portrait verse.
- S2/O1: compiler narration says the Lizong emperor bestowed a gold-bordered Dharma robe and
  title on Wumen Huikai.
- S2/O2: the event heading records a donor presenting a Dharma robe before a hall address; it
  is editorial framing, not the record master's spoken token.

## 27. 拄杖子 (`t_87cc840b8f33`)

All fourteen complete cases support “staff,” repeatedly made the object of direct naming,
questioning, transformation, and demonstration. Eight repeated-token spans were recut to one
actor-pure token. In O4 Yuanwu Keqin, not Yunmen, utters the stored wording while quoting Yunmen.
O3 is recut past the recorder's action marker to Boshan Yuanlai's direct `拄杖子卻辯得`.

## 28. 拈出 (`t_8cf244d2d802`)

All nine complete units support “to bring out / take up and present,” used for a saying, a doubt,
an object offered for inspection, incense, or a claimed treasure. The object varies; the action
does not split into different things. Existing attributions survive full reading: Zhenjing Kewen,
Duanqiao Miaolun, Gaofeng Yuanmiao, Pufeng Haidong, Layman Lu Hang, Tian'an Sheng, Baichi
Yuanshuo, and Yongji Rong utter their tokens; O9 is the unnamed monk's direct request to the master.

## 29. 飯頭 (`t_901f410fce73`)

All seven complete units support the monastic kitchen office, the cook/rice steward. O1–O2 are
two editions of the Huangbo–Linji rice case; the compiler utters the role name while the unnamed
cook performs and speaks. O3–O5 are three records of Yaoshan questioning his unnamed cook. O6
biographically identifies Xuefeng Yicun as the cook under Deshan. O7 is the kitchen inscription's
compact allusion “Xuefeng the cook.” O1's enormous multi-token record span is recut to its first
single actor-pure occurrence.

## 30. 盡大地 (`t_9199b9a31645`)

All eight units support “the whole earth / the entire ground,” with the following predicate doing
different work rather than changing the noun phrase itself. O1 is Yuanwu's commentary quoting
Xuefeng, so Yuanwu—not Xuefeng—is the utterer. O2 and O3 are unnamed monks quoting Xuefeng in
questions to Zhaozhou and Huqiu; both prior Xuefeng MasterNames were wrong. O5 and O7 contained
two parallel tokens and are recut to one. Shangfeng Huihe, Yinyuan Longqi, Yuanwu Keqin, and Yulin
Tongxiu directly utter the remaining examples.

## 31. 無生 (`t_ac4749b5b609`)

All sixteen units support “no arising / unborn,” deployed in questions, definitions, verses, and
encounter formulas. The definition still holds against the enlarged evidence. Three repeated-token
KWICs (Foyin, Chushi, and Guifeng) are recut to one. O12 belongs to Xutang Zhiyu's hall address:
Xutang recites Liangshan Yuanguan's raised case, so Xutang—not Liangshan—is the stored utterer.
O16 remains the unnamed monk's question to Touzi Datong.

## 32. 如何是佛法大意 (`t_bc7bbb4299f1`)

All eight complete units confirm the stock public-interview question “What is the fundamental
meaning of the teaching?” The utterer is always the questioner: Bai Juyi and Ding Shangzuo in two
named cases, otherwise an unnamed monk. O8 is therefore corrected from Linji Yixuan to the unnamed
monk; Linji is the respondent who shouts. Different answers do not create different senses.

## 33. 祖印 (`t_c02887fbd979`)

All seven units support “the ancestral seal,” the publicly claimed authorization or authority of
the ancestral line. Ruibai Mingxue, Kefu Daozhe, and Baoen Shaoan directly utter it. O2–O3 and O6
are questioners asking a presiding master about transmitting or raising it; O7 is the named rear-hall
officer Supu's question to Ruibai. Full reading confirms the existing speaker/respondent distinctions.

## 34. 佛法大意 (`t_cc68e32cf1b4`)

All seven units support the noun phrase “the fundamental meaning of the teaching,” normally inside
the stock question. O1 is Baofu Congzhan's question to Budai. O2, O4, O6, and O7 are unnamed
monks' questions. O3 and O5 are two records of Bai Juyi asking Niaoke Daolin and are corrected to
Bai Juyi; the previous null attribution missed the explicitly named questioner.

## 35. 好手 (`t_d247a2ea7cc3`)

All seven units support “a skilled hand / adept,” an evaluative label for someone effective at the
matter at hand. Full reading overturns the blanket compiler attribution on six cases: Chongyuan
Wenhui, Tiantong Hua, Shoushan Shengnian, Head Seat Cilang, Baichi Yuanshuo, and Zhuyu directly
utter their respective evaluations. O3 alone remains an unattributed verse voice. Named figures
absent from the current roster are preserved as named-unrostered rather than erased.
