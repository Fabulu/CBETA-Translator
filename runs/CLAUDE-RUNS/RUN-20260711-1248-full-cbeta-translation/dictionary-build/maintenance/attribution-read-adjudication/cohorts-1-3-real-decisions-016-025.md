# Cohorts 1–3 genuine full-case decisions — entries 16–25

## 16. 拈香 (`t_a805d0c76bbd`)

The definition holds after all eight complete structural units: to take up incense is the visible
ritual action introducing a dedication, question, lineage declaration, or memorial statement. In
every stored occurrence the headword lies in the compiler's action marker before `云` or `曰`; the
named person performs the act but does not utter the word.

### S1/O1

- Evidence: Konggu Daocheng's full installation record has `拈香云` before four successive direct dedications.
- Ruling: compiler narrator; Konggu is person described/record owner, not headword utterer.

### S1/O2

- Evidence: `公趨前拈香曰` narrates Guo Xiangzheng stepping forward before his direct South Chan dedication.
- Ruling: compiler narrator; Guo is a named lay actor, not a master speaker.

### S1/O3

- Evidence: `師開堂拈香云` opens Shimen Yuncong's record before his direct lineage statement.
- Ruling: compiler narrator; prior Shimen `MasterName` confused action performer with utterer.

### S1/O4

- Evidence: `弟子裕拈香啟問` is the formal preamble to disciple Yu's written Bathing the Buddha questions.
- Ruling: editorial/epistolary narrator, not master speech.

### S1/O5

- Evidence: `遂陞拈香云` precedes Yulin Tongxiu's dedications to benefactors and Tianyin Yuanxiu.
- Ruling: compiler narrator; prior Yulin `MasterName` was an action-performer conflation.

### S1/O6

- Evidence: at the monastery-protector hall, `拈香云` precedes Feiyin Tongrong's direct question.
- Ruling: compiler narrator; prior Feiyin `MasterName` was an action-performer conflation.

### S1/O7

- Evidence: `遂拈香，云：兩彩一賽` narrates Meixi Fudu's action before his compact verdict.
- Ruling: compiler narrator; prior Meixi `MasterName` was an action-performer conflation.

### S1/O8

- Evidence: `師拈香入爐中，曰` narrates Pin Jixiang placing incense in the burner before his memorial challenge.
- Ruling: existing narrated category correct; full unit confirms Pin as named action performer/record owner.

## 17. 方丈 (`t_becc0a1ea8cb`)

The room/person split survives all nine complete units. Movement into or back to 方丈 denotes
the abbot's quarters; human predicates and 方丈和尚 denote the resident abbot. The evidence does
not support an additional abstract “abbacy” sense.

- S1/O1: Xinzhou Zhichang utters the headword in his autobiographical report to Huineng.
- S1/O2: the compiler, not Mazu, narrates Mazu returning to the quarters after Pang bows.
- S1/O3: the compiler, not the unnamed head monk, narrates the head monk going upstairs.
- S1/O4: Chuiwan Guangzhen directly contrasts 方丈地 with 方丈室.
- S1/O5: Baoshou Zhao directly says that innumerable buddhas do not leave these quarters.
- S1/O6: Sihui Miaozhan raises the Yaoshan assembly case inside his own address. He is named
  by the section heading but not yet present in the link roster; Yaoshan is the case figure.
- S2/O1: `璨隱山亦云` explicitly makes Canyin Shan the voice of the ensuing criticism of
  people who occupy 方丈. The prior “unnamed author” ruling was false.
- S2/O2: Baiyu Jingsi directly tells the assembly to ask the resident abbot-monk.
- S2/O3: Xueguan Zhiyin is the author of the titled verse presented to the abbot; the addressee
  is the resident abbot and is not the speaker.

## 18. 竪拂 (`t_df3e128ab4c1`)

not words uttered by the masters who perform the action.

- O1: compiler narration of Nanta Guangyong raising the whisk.
- O2: compiler stage direction; Yuanwu's quoted words begin after `云`. Recut from two headword

- O4: the unnamed monk in Yunmen's raised Zifu case utters the headword in his question.

- O6: compiler stage direction for Baiyu Jingsi; Baiyu's words begin after `云`.

- O8–O9: compiler stage directions in Xueyan Zuqin's hall addresses; his quoted verdicts follow.

## 19. 家珍 (`t_efa921d8f97a`)

All seven units support “household treasure / one's own treasure”: something presented as already
one's own rather than imported through the gate. The definition holds without turning that recurrent
contrast into an external doctrinal equation.

- O1: Zhenjing Kewen recites the old saying and asks what household treasure is. Recut from two
  tokens to the single inherited statement.
- O2: Buhui directly values recognizing one's own household treasure.
- O3: Liao'an Qingyu directly says Mingjue opened the treasury and brought it out.
- O4: genuinely unattributed compiled verse after the Linji dossier; no master is invented.
- O5: Baichi Yuanshuo directly says he brings out the household treasure.
- O6: the old KWIC fused the unnamed monk's questions with Caoshan Qianghui Zhiju's answers and
  contained three tokens. Recut to Caoshan's answer `師曰：不是自家珍。`; Caoshan is the utterer.
- O7: Zhongfeng Mingben directly calls the experienced field one's own household treasure.

## 20. 昭昭靈靈 (`t_faf30cf1fb87`)

All six complete units support the present gloss as vivid or bright awareness, most often named
in order to challenge its identification as the real self or inner master. That recurrent criticism
is a corpus fact, not permission to replace the headword with an outside theory.

- O1–O2: Xuansha Shibei directly questions and explains the expression. Both oversized KWICs
  contained repeated tokens and are recut to one exact Xuansha clause each.
- O3: Zongbao Daodu directly identifies the mistake as taking present bright awareness for the
  answer; recut from two tokens to his first single-token clause.
- O4: Miyun Yuanwu's autobiographical account directly uses the expression for his still-unsettled
  condition before the later change.
- O5: Xueyan Zuqin is the named author of the verse quoted in Tieniu Chiding's biography; the
  headword is in Xueyan's verse, not the biographer's prose or Tieniu's speech.
- O6: the record and nearest headings identify **Yulin Tongxiu**, who directly warns against
  taking the consciousness-spirit as master. The prior Juelang Daosheng attribution was false.

## 22. 禮拜 (`t_1d3473614976`)

does not erase the named action performer from context.

- O2's old span fused the narrator's `豁然大悟禮拜` with Mazu's quoted `禮拜作麼`; it is recut
  to the single narrative action token.
- O1 and O3–O12 remain narrative: Feng, Daliang, unnamed monks, Deshan, a commander, an emperor,

- O13 is different: the complete unit is Poshan Haiming's own hall retelling of the Deshan–Longtan
  case. Poshan utters the stored wording; Deshan Xuanjian is the case figure who bows. The prior
  anonymous-source ruling missed the enclosing address.
