# Non-Iriya v2 semantic canary: adjudication ledger

## Gate and result

The frozen 25 are in `non-iriya-v2-semantic-canary25-selection.md`. Every candidate was read in full-case context in at least two canonical works. Counts below were independently rerun with `zc.count`; each quoted evidence window passed `zc.verify`. Canonical works, not files, control recurrence.

Disposition totals: **13 KEEP, 12 REJECT, 0 PROVISIONAL**. These are review findings only. Nothing here has been admitted to a queue, registry, termbase, or lineage dataset.

Nested-term ruling: a longer string was rejected when it merely adds a narrator/actor to an already covered unit, or when it is an unstable slice through a larger formula. A longer string was retained only when the addition performs a distinct observable Chan job. Diminishing-return ruling: repeated grammatical wrappers do not become headwords merely through frequency.

## Canary-level observations

- Raw recurrence strongly overproduces narrator-plus-action strings: 問如何是、有僧問、僧禮拜、禪師上堂、僧擬議.
- The nested/family gate removed eight high-frequency false headwords without discarding their evidence; it routes that evidence to 如何是、僧問、禮拜、上堂、擬議、拂子/竪起拂子、拄杖 action formulas、衲僧門下、打成一片、趙州勘婆、金剛王寶劍 as applicable.
- High-yield discoveries were concentrated in public-interview operators and institutional modes (請上堂、舉僧問、問話者、眾下語、請普說) and in formulaic tests/verdicts (第一義、正恁麼時、信得及、三千里外).
- 光明藏 needs title-versus-phrase sense hygiene during entry building; KEEP does not authorize merging those two things.

**STOP GATE:** independent review is required before expanding beyond these 25 or mutating any authoritative artifact.
