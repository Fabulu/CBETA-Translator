## 立雪 — `t_d2892b1eaae0`

### Final sense structure

1. **Preferred, Zen-loaded case sense:** “Huike's standing in the snow” / “the standing-in-snow vigil.” This is the compact name of Huike's lineage encounter with Bodhidharma. Keep all five current occurrences here: full narrative, standing-snow/cut-arm formula, later praise and criticism, and the public-interview question.
2. **Allowed physical-action sense:** “to stand in snow.” This is the compositional posture of a heron in snow, not Huike's historical event. The same heron image can be used as an interview answer or comparison; those are deployments of one physical posture, not further senses.

The current explanation incorrectly says the heron uses “do not warrant a second sense.” They do: “Huike's standing-in-snow case” and “a heron stands in snow” refer to different things. Conflicting appraisals of Huike's event remain readings inside sense 1.

### New anchors for physical-action sense

1. `T/T51/T51n2077.xml`, `0480b05–0480b06`
   - KWIC: `問四威儀中如何履踐。師曰。鷺鶿立雪。曰恁麼則聞鐘持鉢日上闌干。`
   - English: “Asked, ‘How does one proceed within the four deportments?’ the master said, ‘A heron stands in the snow.’”
   - Why unique: direct subject-predicate use with an animal subject; it cannot denote Huike's vigil.

2. `X/X81/X81n1568.xml`, `0098b08–0098b09`
   - KWIC: `鷺鷥立雪非同色，明月蘆花不似他。`
   - English: “A heron standing in snow is not of the same color; the bright moon and reed flowers are not alike.”
   - Why unique: a recurrent comparison built from the visible bird-and-snow scene, distinct from the direct-answer syntax above.

### Exclusions/family decision

- Do not create separate senses for `立雪斷臂`, `二祖立雪`, `立雪齊腰`, praise, criticism, or later questioning: all name or appraise the same Huike event.
- `鷺鷥` and `鷺鶿` are graphic forms in the heron family, not separate senses of 立雪.
- Keep links to 斷臂, 面壁, 傳衣, 慧可, and 達磨; add 鷺鷥/鷺鶿 as physical-family evidence.

## 普說 — `t_bb19ed0e0fab`

### Final sense structure

1. **Preferred noun/event sense:** “general address” / “public general address.” This covers the scheduled genre, headings naming an address, its placard, drum, venue, and occasion. A heading and the event it labels are the same thing.
2. **Allowed verb sense:** “to give a general address” / “address the community generally.” This requires a speaker and can take an audience or object (`普說此`, “address this”; `為諸人普說一上`, “give the people an address”).

Reassign the current `T/T47/T47n1998A.xml` and `X/X70/X70n1403.xml` request constructions to the verbal sense: both describe someone being requested to deliver the address. Keep the classification, placard/procedure, and `因事普說` heading under the noun/event sense. That yields 3 noun + 4 verb occurrences after the additions.

### New anchors for verbal sense

   - Why unique: `此` is the expressed object of the verbal phrase; this is not merely an event heading.

   - English: “I will give you all one general address; you should regard it as a rare encounter.”

### Exclusions/family decision

- Do not split `普說` headings from the address event.
- Do not merge it with 上堂, 小參, 示眾, 問答, or 秉拂. The corpus lists them separately and gives the general address its own procedure.

## 作家 — `t_dab856504b69`

### Poet-colligation adjudication: **do not split**

`作家詩客` has 7 hits in 7 allowlisted files, but all seven are witnesses of the **same transmitted encounter** between a visitor and Dayu Zhi. The surrounding sequence is stable:

1. the visitor says, “When one meets a swordsman, one must present a sword; unless one meets a poet, one does not offer a poem” (`路逢劍客須呈劍，不是詩人不獻詩`);
2. Dayu replies `作家詩客`, “an accomplished/expert poet”;
3. the visitor answers, “One red thread is pulled by two people.”

No `詩客作家`, `作家詞客`, `作家騷客`, or `詩中作家` family was found. The colligation is therefore not productive evidence for an independent “author/writer” sense. More importantly, 作家 contributes the same meaning in `作家詩客`, `作家宗師`, `作家戰將`, and `作家禪客`: a person genuinely accomplished in the named role. Poet and Chan master are different people/domains, but the word itself denotes the same expert status. Splitting by profession would violate item 8's ban on splitting different readings/applications of the same thing.

### Final one-sense structure

Retain one corpus-wide sense, preferably **“an adept; an expert hand”**, with alternates “a real hand” and “accomplished.” Rewrite the current claim “In Chan…” so it does not falsely restrict the word to Chan operators: the corpus overwhelmingly applies it to tested Chan competence, but one transmitted exchange applies the same expert adjective to a poet. Explicitly forbid the modern default “author/writer.”

### Three new anchors (total 8)

1. **Direct interview definition:** `J/J28/J28nB220.xml`, `0781b11–0781b12`
   - KWIC: `鉗鎚佛祖，必須作家。如何是作家？」師云：「殺人不眨眼。」`
   - English: “‘To hammer buddhas and patriarchs requires an adept. What is an adept?’ The master said, ‘One who kills without blinking.’”
   - Why unique: exact `如何是作家` definition-question, absent from the current occurrence set.

2. **Negative public verdict:** `B/B14/B14n0082.xml`, `0275b12`
   - KWIC: `時有僧出礼拜師云不是作家出去`
   - English: “A monk came forward and bowed. The master said, ‘Not an adept—out!’”
   - Why unique: shows 作家 as a competence verdict that can be denied in the public interview.

3. **Poet application within the same expert sense:** `T/T51/T51n2077.xml`, `0485c24–0485c26`
   - KWIC: `師曰。路逢劍客須呈劍。不是詩人不獻詩。曰作家詩客。師曰。一條紅線兩人牽。`
   - English: “The master said, ‘When one meets a swordsman, one must present a sword; unless one meets a poet, one does not offer a poem.’ [Dayu] said, ‘An accomplished poet.’ The master said, ‘One red thread is pulled by two people.’”
   - Why unique: forces the definition to cover expert status outside the Chan-master role while disproving a separate modern “author” sense.

### Exclusions/family decision

- Do not create “author” or “writer.” The corpus evidence says poet (`詩客`) in one repeatedly transmitted case.
- `作家宗師`, `作家禪客`, `作家戰將`, `作家相見`, `作家手段`, positive praise, and negative verdicts all share the expert/real-hand sense.
- Keep 宗師, 禪客, 作家相見, 本分, and competence-testing public-interview language in the family.
