# Prose Hygiene and Calibration Pass

Status: **mandatory retrospective and future-entry gate**  
User calibration: 2026-07-15

## Target standard

Use the accepted fresh entries `鳥道` (`t_462d9613abe9`) and `和尚`
(`t_8f7b20536cb6`) as the positive calibration pair. They are models of method,
not prose templates to copy.

### What `鳥道` gets right

- It pictures the ordinary scene first: a bird crosses open air without leaving
  a ground-track on which another traveler can set a foot.
- It then surfaces the corpus-earned Chan bend instead of stopping at the graph
  calque “bird path.”
- It tests the tempting inference “a non-path that cannot be followed” against
  counterevidence such as `行鳥道`, `鳥道難行`, and human traversal.
- It splits a genuinely different thing—the precipitous mountain trail—from the
  trackless flight-course.
- It reports unresolved and residual concordance buckets honestly instead of
  forcing every hit into a preferred reading.
- Its search aliases let an English reader find the article through road/path/
  way/course language without collapsing the two senses.

### What `和尚` gets right

- It separates the general respectful title “master” from the institutionally
  distinct ordination-preceptor role.
- It distinguishes direct address, honorific naming, compiler narration, and a
  named non-master’s utterance.
- It explains why a visually similar occurrence (Wuzu Jie, where 戒 is a name)
  does **not** establish the ordination sense.
- Each evidence note names the source and the exact actor, or explicitly names
  the documentary/narrative owner where no person utters the headword.

## Mandatory prose-hygiene questions

Apply these to **every accepted entry retrospectively** and every new entry
before independent KEEP:

1. Can an English reader picture or identify the ordinary thing after the first
   two sentences?
2. Does the article say where the Chan corpus bends, institutionalizes, tests,
   rebukes with, or otherwise redeploys the word?
3. Does every important inference name its stored evidence rather than merely
   announcing that “the records use” the expression?
4. Was the attractive interpretation tested against counterexamples and
   negative results?
5. Are different referents split, while grammatical variants and paraphrases of
   the same referent remain one sense?
6. Are unresolved hits reported as unresolved rather than silently recruited?
7. Are aliases useful English retrieval paths without smuggling in an
   unsupported interpretation?
8. Does prose name the exact speaker, narrator, questioner, actor, or documented
   impersonal construction rather than saying “a master,” “the source voice,”
   or “the text”?
9. Does every source note name the actual work, not merely “Source Record”?
10. Could any paragraph be pasted unchanged under a different headword? If yes,
    it is template filler and must be rewritten.

## Retrospective pass

The pass covers the full accepted fresh termbase, including entries already
marked `STATUS=done`. Passing mechanics or a prior KEEP does not exempt prose.

Prioritize shared openings; calque-first and opaque concrete-image entries;
generic deployment inventories; vague source/actor prose; high-frequency and
keystone entries; hidden referent splits; and unsupported or redundant aliases.

Every changed entry must be reopened, compiled from its worksheet, pass exact
KWIC/attribution/depth/count/work-source/public-feedback gates, receive an
independent exact-hash semantic rereview, and only then return to `STATUS=done`.
Never bulk-polish generated artifacts or hand-edit merged/site files.
