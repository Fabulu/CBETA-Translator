## Notes for the author
- Tier A is the priority cluster: `石女 / 木人 / 木馬 / 泥牛 / 鐵牛` form one interlocking koan-menagerie
  (they co-occur in the same lines) — consider cross-`RelatedTerms` links. `露地白牛`/`水牯牛` (already in
  termbase) are the buffalo cousins; relate to them too.
- `毒蛇` (234/110) was CONSIDERED and set aside: its main attestation is 四大毒蛇 = the four elements, a
  standard-Buddhist gloss, not a Zen deviation. `死蛇`/`活蛇` carry the Zen-specific load instead.
- `師子/獅子` bare (3850+3230) NOT listed: as the lion's-roar it is standard Buddhist, not a Zen bend.
  The deviation lives in `師子兒` (test-name) and `金毛師子` (master-as-lion), which are listed.
- Disambiguation flags for the author: `象王`, `金翅鳥`, `香象` also appear in plain sūtra/cosmology
  contexts — the entry must isolate the Chan deployment (the koan/gaze/fording image) from ordinary use.
  `黃龍` was dropped: overwhelmingly a master/place name, not a creature-metaphor.
- KWICs above are exact contiguous substrings pulled via zc.find; re-anchor with them (per guide #5).
