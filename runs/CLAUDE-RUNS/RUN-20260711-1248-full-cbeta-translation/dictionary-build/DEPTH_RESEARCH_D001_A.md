## 上堂 (`t_4f7bd98ad40f`)

### Re-tested definition and family

The existing article itself says the headword names both the physical act and “the formal address that results.” These are different things under item 8. Recommended order:

1. **formal teaching-hall address / observance** — the Zen-institutional primary sense; includes headings, requested occasions, scheduled observances, raised cases, and public questions;
2. **ascend the hall / take the teaching seat** — the physical action, already anchored by `府主王常侍與諸官請師升座，師上堂`.

`小參` remains a different entry and format: its code evidence says it has no fixed place and calls it the “family teaching.” It is a contrast, not another 上堂 sense. `陞座/升座` overlaps with the physical sense but does not erase the formal-address sense. The existing inaugural `入寺上堂`, pause formula, sponsor, and placard anchors should remain.

## 恁麼 (`t_7180f7431520`)

### Re-tested definition and family

Keep one sense: **thus / like this / in this way**. Coming thus, going thus, understanding thus, conditional “if thus,” and a conclusion introduced by 恁麼則 all preserve the same deictic. `與麼` is a graphic variant, not another thing. `恁麼則` has its own entry because the whole fixed connective means “if so, then”; that family entry is compatible with, and should be linked from, the base article.

No stable title/person or master-specific referent was found. Zen bends an ordinary demonstrative by making it the recurrent pointer in public interviews, especially when a student's inference is immediately tested or rejected; the graph meaning does not change.

### Missing deployments and verified candidates

1. **Student's inference turn (`恁麼則`)** — distinct dialogue grammar, with the master reversing the proposed inference.  
   `X/X80/X80n1565.xml`, `0053b18–0053b19`  
   `曰。恁麼則順正歸元去也。師曰。歸元何順。`

2. **Appraisal of “understanding it thus”** — shows the demonstrative modifying a proposed understanding and receiving the verdict “missed it.”  
   `T/T48/T48n2003.xml`, `0141c09`  
   `纔恁麼會。蹉過了也。`

3. **Moment formula (`正當恁麼時`)** — temporal deictic followed by a public demand.  
   `T/T47/T47n1997.xml`, `0723b09–0723b10`  
   `且道。正當恁麼時如何。蟬聲時到耳。雁影忽迎眸。`

4. **Conditional instruction (`若恁麼`)** — conditional deployment rather than inference or reported understanding.  
   `T/T48/T48n2003.xml`, `0140b02–0140b03`  
   `若恁麼見得。便有自由分。不隨一切語言轉。`

5. **Doubled positive/negative citation** — corpus-internal evidence for the productive opposition, not a new sense.  
   `C/C077/C077n1710.xml`, `0823a02`

6. **A proposed understanding tested by a follow-up question** — preserves the public-interview response shape rather than merely listing a collocation.  
   `X/X82/X82n1571.xml`, `0006a17–0006a18`  
   `曰：便恁麼會時如何？師曰：迢迢十萬不是遠。`

### Omission audit

The combined evidence covers arrival, departure, impossibility (`恁麼/不恁麼總不得`), graphic variant 與麼, inference, understanding, exact moment, condition, doubling, and a tested response. More occurrences of the extremely frequent connective would be padding unless they establish a new syntactic construction.

## 作麼生 (`t_51fe593d9ffe`)

### Re-tested definition and family

Keep one corpus-wide interrogative sense: **how? / in what way? / what about it?** The object questioned changes, but the expression remains an interrogative. `作麼生道` is a narrower compound/family entry: it specifically demands or reports speech, while bare 作麼生 can press for an act, understanding, further response, or normative alternative. `作勿生`, `作摩生`, and `怎麼生` are historically/graphically different forms of the same interrogative, not item-8 splits.

The Zen deviation is the expression's pervasive public-interview function: it repeatedly returns responsibility for the next word or act to the interlocutor. This is an observable dialogue role, not an interpretation of intent.

## 示眾 (`t_1a7e251bda53`)

### Re-tested definition and family

Confirmed item-8 split. Recommended order:

1. **address / instruct the assembly** — the Zen public-discourse format, including spoken instruction, formal headings, and final instructions;
2. **show / display to the assembly** — physically present an object, including the flower, whisk, garment, or mirror.

The two senses share the assembly as audience but differ in the act performed. `上堂` and `小參` are neighboring institutional formats, not senses of 示眾; the corpus itself groups and contrasts them. `拈花示眾` belongs to the physical-display sense and is the defining Zen-Buddha deployment. `舉拂示眾` connects the physical sense to the teaching-seat implement without assigning a symbolic meaning to the whisk.

   `J/J25/J25nB163.xml`, `0256c01`  
   `洛浦臨終示眾云：「今有一事問你諸人`

2. **Whisk displayed to the assembly** — second physical-sense anchor and teaching-seat implement family.  
   `T/T47/T47n1997.xml`, `0715c08`  
   `舉拂示眾云。大眾見麼。`

3. **Garment physically raised for a public question** — a non-flower/non-whisk display, proving the sense is productive rather than a single fixed case.  
   `X/X80/X80n1565.xml`, `0114a16–0114a17`  
   `藥山提起示眾曰。法身還具四大也無。`

4. **Mirror physically raised before the assembly** — display within an ascent-to-the-hall event and a teacher-to-successor exchange.

The resulting set gives both senses multiple anchors: the verbal side includes ascent-to-seat instruction, grouped genre, Huangbo's address, the small-convocation comparison, final instruction, and a spoken declaration; the physical side includes flower, whisk, garment, and mirror. Further displayed objects would repeat the productive physical pattern.

## 法嗣 (`t_8a06e7d99b19`)

### Re-tested definition and family

The enrichment exposes an item-8 split that the current single target hides:

1. **lineage heir / Dharma successor** — a person, as in teacher headings, counted heirs, `誰之法嗣`, absence of an heir, and lay successors;
2. **Dharma succession / lineage affiliation** — the relation or status, as in `得法嗣何人` (from whom did one receive/succeed to the Dharma), `未詳法嗣者` (persons whose succession affiliation is undetailed), and `法嗣書` (succession document).

The current explanation sometimes paraphrases `得法嗣` as “obtain lineage heirs”; the recurrent question `師今得法嗣何人` shows that this cannot be preserved globally. `嗣法` remains the separate verbal family entry “inherit the lineage.” Its `嗣法於X` construction is compatible with the abstract-relation sense of 法嗣 but should not be collapsed into this headword.

### Missing deployments and verified candidates

1. **Direct person-role test: whose lineage heir?** — unambiguous heir/person sense.  
   `J/J26/J26nB180.xml`, `0291b24–0291b25`  
   `上云：「無念和尚誰之法嗣？」`

2. **Succession received from whom** — abstract relation/affiliation sense; corrects the current global “obtain heirs” reading.  
   `X/X78/X78n1553.xml`, `0495b13`  
   `問：祖祖相傳傳祖印，師今得法嗣何人？`

3. **Explicit absence of an heir** — negative person-role deployment and a retrospective comparison.  
   `J/J29/J29nB238.xml`, `0466a16–0466a17`  
   `寧效無法嗣之睦州、不出頭之清素`

4. **Lay lineage succession** — proves the person-role is not limited to ordained monks.  
   `X/X80/X80n1566.xml`, `0450b13–0450b14`  
   `密菴何居士法嗣素菴田大士`

5. **Counted collateral heir in a lamp genealogy** — distinct catalogue structure, not merely another master's ordinary heading.  
   `X/X79/X79n1557.xml`, `0001b08`  
   `四祖道信大師旁出法嗣一人`

6. **Succession document (`法嗣書`)** — institutional object derived from the abstract relation, not from “heir” as a person.  
   `C/C077/C077n1710.xml`, `0783c15–0783c16`  
   `專使文詳持法嗣書到師於法座前受書`

### Omission audit

The resulting set covers lineage headings, recognition, prediction, strict limitation, unknown affiliation, direct “whose heir?” grammar, received succession, no heir, lay succession, collateral enumeration, and a succession document. More one-/two-/sixteen-person headings would duplicate the counted-heading class. During rewrite, move `未詳法嗣者` to the abstract relation sense and ensure at least one occurrence remains under each sense.
